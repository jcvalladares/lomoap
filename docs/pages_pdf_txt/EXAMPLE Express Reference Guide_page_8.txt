Notifications
The Notifications page allows users to send and receive important announcements or informations. Notifications can
be generated by individual users, or by the system using ManuScript logic. Notifications can be associated with specific
policies (for example, John Doe's policy has been approved), or can convey general announcements that are not
policy-specific (for example, Agents in Territory 31 cannot write Personal Auto policies through 1/1/11).
Notifications can be sent to individual users or to groups of users. EXAMPLE Express ®tracks the status of each
notification per user, allowing notifications to be tracked as read or unread for each user. In addition, notifications can
be managed through batch processing.
