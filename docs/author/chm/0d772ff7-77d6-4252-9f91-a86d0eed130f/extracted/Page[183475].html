Navigating to Parent and Ancestor Groups

Save Page to .PDF

Fields View

In complex ManuScripts, you can quickly navigate to parent or ancestor groups.

To select the parent group of the active field or group:

  1. Click the appropriate field or group.
  2. On the Edit menu, click Go to Parent.

    You can also:
    • Press F10.
    • Right-click the field or group and then click Go to Parent.

To select the first public ancestor group of the active field or group:

  1. Click the appropriate field or group.
  2. On the Edit menu, click Go to Public Parent.

    You can also:
    • Press F11.
    • Right-click the field or group and then click Go to Public Parent.
