Forms Mapper

Save Section to .PDF Save Page to .PDF

You use the Forms Mapper to create and edit forms. With Forms Mapper, you can define:

  • Properties that describe how and when a form or sub-form is generated.
  • Merge fields that associate ManuScript fields with specific locations on a form.
  • Formats for information that appears on a form.
  • Settings for the groups through which the program iterates for a form or sub-form.
  • Watermarks and stamps to add to a form (when generating .pdf output files).
  • Print jobs (specific sets of forms).

You can define these items through four different components of Forms Mapper:

  • Forms
  • Sub-forms
  • Merge fields
  • Groups

To open the Forms Mapper view:

  • Click the Forms Mapper button  .

    You can also:
    On the View menu, click Forms Mapper.
