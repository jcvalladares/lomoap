Viewing Forms Inheritance Viewing Forms Inheritance Save Page to .PDF Forms Mapper View You can view the ManuScript inheritance chain for forms in the current ManuScript in the Inheritance dialog box. The Inheritance (Forms) dialog box displays the entire ManuScript inheritance chain for the current ManuScript's forms. From this dialog box, you can open any ManuScript in the inheritance chain by selecting the desired ManuScript and clicking Go to . You can access the Inheritance (Forms) dialog box from Forms Mapper in two ways: On the View menu, point to Inheritance , and then click Forms . On the shortcut menu for Forms Mapper, click View Inheritance (Forms) .
