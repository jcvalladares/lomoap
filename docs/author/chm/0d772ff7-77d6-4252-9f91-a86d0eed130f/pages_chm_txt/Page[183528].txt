Viewing Table Inheritance Viewing Table Inheritance Save Page to .PDF Tables View You can view the ManuScript inheritance chain for tables in the current ManuScript in the Inheritance dialog box. The Inheritance (Data) dialog box displays the entire ManuScript inheritance chain for the current ManuScript's data (fields and tables). From this dialog box, you can open any ManuScript in the inheritance chain by selecting the desired ManuScript and clicking Go to . You can access the Inheritance (Data) dialog box from Tables view in two ways: On the View menu, point to Inheritance , and then click Data . On the shortcut menu for Tables View, click View Inheritance (Data) .
