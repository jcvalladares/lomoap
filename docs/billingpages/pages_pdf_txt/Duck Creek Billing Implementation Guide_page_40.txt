The Account Home page appears and when the MPR form is generated for an account, the activity is included in the
Recent Account Activity list for the account.
Producing the Form
A forms ManuScript must be created for each line of business. Copy the Billing_MPR_Forms_Line1 ManuScript and
adjust the keys and IDs for the target line of business. This ManuScript can be customized with your specific form and
data. The EXAMPLE Server ®code calls the <billing.printFormsRq> request to initiate the production of the form and
creating the activity log entry.
After the first MPR form is created, the next form is automatically scheduled along with a scheduled activity to evaluate
PCNR for non-reporting or PCNP for non-payment of a report. EXAMPLE Server ®schedules the next MPR activity by
using the due day of the account, subtracting the MPRPrdOffset value, and backing up the date if necessary for non-
processing days.
To prevent scheduling conflicts, verify the calculated production date is before the last allowed date
(LastAvailMPRDtDisp value).
Processing the Returned MPR Form
MPR Report data is entered from the Policy Term Summary page for the appropriate account in Duck Creek Billing™.
SeeWorking with Monthly Premium Reporting in the Duck Creek Billing™ User Guide . The collection ManuScript must
be created, and can be modeled after the out-of-the-box sample MPR Collection Line 1 ManuScript that works with
BaseProduct.
Pending Cancel for Non-Reporting
A PCNR follow-up is similar to a regular PCN for non-payment. Policy term parameters control whether the policy can
PCNR for non-reporting or PCNP for non-payment of the MPR receivable.
Final Audit
When a Final Audit transaction is received for an MPR policy by Duck Creek Billing™, a new receivable transaction for
the entire premium amount replaces any existing premium receivables for the policy term.
The only transactions valid in Duck Creek Billing™ following a Final Audit are Revised Audit or Void Final
Audit.
For details about entering MPR Report information, see Working with Monthly Premium Reporting in the Duck Creek
Billing™ User Guide .
For more information about configuring an MPR policy, contact your Account Representative.
