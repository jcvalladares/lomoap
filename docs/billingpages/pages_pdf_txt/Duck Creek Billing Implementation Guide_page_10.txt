The Billing Dashboard
From the Billing Dashboard page, a user can:
•Search for a payment.
•Enter a single payment from the Cash Management screen instead of within an account.
•View summary information for payments automatically or manually entered that were not posted to a policy or
account.
•Create an account in the billing system without going through policy issuance.
•View a list of recently created or accessed accounts.
•Open an available report with one click.
•View notifications and tasks.
