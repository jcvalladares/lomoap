Selecting Billing Account Options
Billing plans may be configured by specifying the values for Billing Account Options. The available options control all
automated billing and collection processes, including account type, installment scheduling and invoicing and collection
follow-up activities. The options Duck Creek Billing™ users see is determined by the ManuScript configuration.
Any modifications to existing Billing Account Option configurations will affect all billing accounts and policies
which reference the configuration.
