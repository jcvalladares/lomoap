The Account Home Page
The Account Home page is the primary workhorse for the Duck Creek Billing™ system, and provides a direct path for
accessing and managing billing activities at the account level.
The Account Home page provides quick access to view and work with:
•The most recent invoice.
•The account history, sortable by date, activity type, or policy term in the Recent Account Activity list.
•A list of all policy terms associated with the account, with a link for quick access to the Policy Term Summary
page for each term.
•A summary of the account ledger and account details, providing a quick look at the account status.
