Policy Term Configuration
The following table lists the additional policy plan parameters required to indicate a policy is configured for MPR.
Field Name Definition
CollectionMSID The ManuScript ID of the LOB-specific collection screen.
FormMSID The ManuScript ID of the LOB-specific forms ManuScript.
MPRDueDay The day the MPR completion is due.
MPRPrdOffset The number of days before the MPR due date that the MPR form is produced.
PCNPFollowUp The number of days after the MPR due date that a PCNP follow-up activity is scheduled.
PCNRTrigDays The number of days after the MPR due date that a PCNR follow-up activity is triggered.
Allow PCNNP Indicates whether a policy should PCN for non-payment of an MPR receivable.
AllowPCNNR Indicates whether a policy should PCN for non-reporting.
LastAvailMPRDtDisp Controls when the last MPR form can be produced for a policy.
Creating an MPR Policy
Because customization is required to utilize the MPR feature, the steps required to create a policy will vary. The
following procedure is based on out-of-the-box functionality, and assumes the appropriate ManuScripts have been
customized.
To create an MPR policy:
1.Click the Policy Management tab.
2.Create an auditable policy with a Monthly audit period frequency.
3.Enter the policy information.
4.Make note of the policy number before leaving the Policy Management system.
5.On the Submission page, select the MPR Policy check box.
6.Click Complete Issuance .
7.Click the Billing tab.
8.From the Billing Dashboard, in the I want to find an account by list, click one of the following options:
◦Account Number
◦Policy Number
9.In the searching for text box, enter the policy number noted in Step 4.
10. Click Search .
