Manually Redistributing Installments
Manual installment redistribution allows customers to have additional time to pay a large amount due, such as final
audit premiums. When configured, this feature is accessible from the Installment Schedule page as a link in the
Balance column. The link is only available when the Installment Schedule view is expanded, an open balance exists,
and the Account Type Code does not equal Agent .
Installments can be manually redistributed outside the current policy term dates, and any items that have been
redistributed outside the policy term are catch-up items on the next installment.
To have the required permissions to manually redistribute an installment, the logged in user must be
assigned the role of BillingAgent with the BIL_ManualInstallmentRedistribution privilege. This is an out-of-the-
box privilege assigned to the BillingAgent role. For more information on billing roles and privileges, see
Creating a Billing Role in the Duck Creek Billing™ User Guide .
Creating a New EXAMPLE Server ®Request
The <BillSchedule.redistributeInstallmentBalanceRq> request must be created to control the redistribution. This
request must specify the BillItemScheduleID , the earliest installment date for the redistribution, and the number of new
installments to create.
Redistributed installments will have a different installment type code than regular installments so they can be easily
identified in the database tables. This change also allows credit allocation to work correctly since the usual method of
matching debits based on effective and expiration dates will not work with redistributed installments.
The existing credit allocation processing will be changed to search for debits that could be outside the range of
installment dates based on their effective and expiration dates.
Working with a Late Entered Policy
Through ManuScript configuration, the Late Entered Strategy feature allows a Duck Creek Billing™ user to insert an
out-of-cycle draw for an EFT invoice account for a late entered new business policy. Out of the box, without the Late
Entered Strategy feature enabled, when the scheduled invoice day has already been passed in the current month, a
late entered new business policy installment is added to the next scheduled installment for the account, resulting in as
much as 31 days of coverage without premium payment.
TheLate Entered Strategy feature has a positive effect on cash flow and commission authorization, in addition to
protecting policy equity by minimizing the amount of time coverage is provided without payment.
TheLate Entered Strategy is valid for a new business premium by EFT invoice only. Other transactions, such
as endorsements and renewals, are scheduled based on the Late Entered Strategy Code value on the policy
plan associated with the policy. For more information, see Selecting Policy Plan Options .
