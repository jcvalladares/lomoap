1.From the Account Home page, click Items in Suspense .
2.Select the check box for the appropriate item in suspense.
3.In the Select An Action list, click Disburse .
4.Select one of the following check boxes:
◦Refund To Check
◦Refund To EFT
5.Click Submit .
6.(Optional) Click Select Another Payee to name a different payee for the disbursement.
7.Select the appropriate party.
8.(Optional) In the Disbursement Amount text box, enter an amount other than the default to disburse less than
the full amount.
9.Click Refund .
10. (Optional) Click the View Details link to see the details of the item pending disbursement.
