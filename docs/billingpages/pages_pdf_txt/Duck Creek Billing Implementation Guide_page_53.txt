To add a new commission plan:
1.From the Active Commission Plans page, click Add New Commission Plan .
The Commission Plan Details page appears.
2.Accept the default selections, or use the dropdown list or text box to enter any different values.
3.Enter the plan name in the Description text box.
4.In the Terms section, below the Commission Rate heading, click one of the following options in the Type list:
◦Percentage
◦Flat Amount
5.In the Value text box, enter the appropriate amount.
6.Below the Commission Distribution heading, click one of the available options in the Entity Type list.
The available options are determined by ManuScript customization.
7.In the Percentage orAmount text box, enter the appropriate amount.
Enter percentages as numbers carried out to two decimal points. For example, 10 percent would be
entered as 10.00.
8.After entering the value, click the page outside of the text box to refresh the page, or tab outside the text box.
9.(Optional) To add an additional distribution for the commission amount, click Add New Distribution .
10. (Optional) Repeat Steps 6 and 7 for each additional distribution.
11. (Optional) Click Delete Distribution to remove a distribution from the commission plan.
12. Click the page outside of the text box to refresh the page.
The percentage or amount for other entity types will need to be changed to match the specified
commission rate amount.
