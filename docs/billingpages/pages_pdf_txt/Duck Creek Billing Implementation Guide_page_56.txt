Configuring Commissions
The commission scheme for a policy is specified from the Policy Term Settings for the appropriate account. See
Working with Commissions in the Duck Creek Billing™ User Guide .
In addition to selecting the commission scheme option, ManuScript customization is required for commission
configuration.
