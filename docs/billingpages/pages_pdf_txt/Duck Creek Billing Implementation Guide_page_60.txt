Working with the Commission Statement Maintenance Page
The Commission Statement Maintenance page provides quick user access for working with commission statements for
an account.
The Commission Statement Maintenance page allows the user to:
•View the details of a pending commission statement
•Stop or resume the production of a pending commission statement
•Create an ad hoc (on demand) commission statement
•Manually adjust amounts on the pending commission statement
The commission statement includes the following information:
•Policyholder Name
•Gross Premium Amount
•Commission Rate or Flat Amount
•The Seller (Producer) Reference
•Seller (Producer) Name
•Authorized commission that has not been paid for the current commission statement period
•Authorized commission that has been reversed, or clawed back, either manually or automatically
(An automatic adjustment is triggered in response to a policy premium transaction)
(A m anual adjustment is initiated by a user and is not triggered in response to a policy premium transaction)
•Authorized commission that has been paid, but the payment was returned
The Commission Statement Maintenance page includes only items associated with the current statement period. A
debit balance carries forward as a single entry into the next commission statement period. To reduce or eliminate a
debit balance, an agent payment can be entered from the Make A Payment page for the appropriate account, or be
entered as a single payment from the Billing Dashboard.
