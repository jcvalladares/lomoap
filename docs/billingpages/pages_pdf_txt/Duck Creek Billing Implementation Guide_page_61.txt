Holding or Resuming Statement Production
When the production of a commission statement is placed on hold, it is for an indefinite period of time. The hold must
be manually released to resume the commission statement production. Holding and resuming statement production is
added to the Recent Account Activity list for the agent's account, but not all the details are listed. If an agent is
terminated or canceled, commission statement generation does not automatically stop, but can be manually
suspended using the hold statement feature.
To place a commission statement on hold:
1.On the Commission Statement Maintenance page, in the Hold Reason list, click the appropriate option.
Out of the box, the available options are:
•Reason 1
•Reason 2
•Reason 3
The text for available options may be customized in the
Carrier_Billing_Commission_Statement_Authorization_Maintenance_1_0_0_0.xml ManuScript.
TheHold Statement button appears.
2.Click Hold Statement .
The statement will remain on hold until statement activity is manually resumed.
To resume statement production:
•Click Resume Statement .
