# Wichita Public Schools Case Study

## Overview
Wichita Public Schools, a large school district serving approximately 45,000 students across 97 schools, special program sites, and support centers, experienced significant challenges with their legacy claims management system. This led them to transition to Origami Risk, a cloud-based platform that provided scalability, security, and ease of use.

### Key Challenges
1. **Legacy System Lacks Scalability**: The existing system was outdated and inefficient.
2. **Incompatibility with New Systems**: Integrating with newer systems required extensive manual workarounds.
3. **Manual Data Entry and Spreadsheet Tracking**: Increased risk of missed deadlines, miscoded injuries, and incomplete information.
4. **Existing Combination of Programs and Data**: Complex and fragmented data in different systems required manual integration.
5. **Budget Constraints**: Cost-effective but robust solution needed.

### Solutions
Wichita Public Schools implemented Origami Risk for claims management and retirement data tracking. They transitioned to a scalable platform and intuitive user experience, which allowed them to stay budget-conscious while meeting the needs of busy educators.

### Streamlined Workflows
Origami Risk streamlined workflows by eliminating manual re-entry and improving productivity by several hours each week. It also significantly shortened the time to process and report incidents. The system provided teachers with valuable in-class time while ensuring more complete documentation.

### Expanded Risk Management Horizon
The system expanded the risk management horizon by integrating with Wichita Public School's position management system to ensure timely recruitment actions, reducing delays and errors in staffing transitions. It also provided opportunities for safety audits, facility evaluations, and preventive risk tracking.

### Future Goals
The team is looking to use Origami Risk for additional risk assessments, facility evaluations, and preventive risk tracking. They aim to develop dashboard development for district-wide trend analysis and further automate workflows to further improve Wichita Public Schools.

## Conclusion
Wichita Public Schools successfully transitioned to Origami Risk, leveraging its scalability, security, and ease of use to enhance their claims management and retirement data tracking. By implementing Origami Risk, they were able to streamline workflows, reduce costs, and improve overall efficiency. This case study demonstrates the benefits of using a cloud-based platform for risk and insurance management.
