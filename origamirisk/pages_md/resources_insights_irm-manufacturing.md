# Origami Risk: A Comprehensive Platform for Risk Management and Safety

## Overview
Origami Risk is a platform designed to enhance operations by providing a flexible, scalable, and secure environment for managing insurable risks, environmental, health and safety compliance, and governance processes. The platform offers customizable solutions for risk, safety, and insurance, including cloud-native tools, mobile apps, and comprehensive reporting and analytics capabilities.
