# Smarter, Safer, Faster: The Future of Risk Management in Manufacturing

## Summary

Origami Risk is an innovative platform designed to enhance operations with speed, scalability, and security. The platform offers AI tools to boost speed, insight, and impact, seamless integration with data sources, expert system configuration and guidance, and intuitive app for on-the-go reporting. It provides purpose-built solutions for risk, safety, and insurance, including cloud-native tools for policy, billing, and claims. The platform also offers centralizing and managing insurable risk data, operational risk management, governance, risk and compliance, business continuity management, internal audit, internal controls management, policy & procedure management, and third-party risk management. The platform is designed for industries such as P&C Insurance, Construction, Government, Healthcare, Manufacturing, and Transportation.
