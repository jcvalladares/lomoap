# Origami Risk: Comprehensive Exposure Management Software

## Overview
Origami Risk is a cloud-based platform designed to enhance insurance operations through advanced exposure management capabilities. With its innovative platform, users can streamline exposure value collection, monitoring, and forecasting, leading to more confident coverage decisions and improved overall risk management outcomes.

## Key Features
### Exposure Tracking and Forecasting
- **Centralized Record Keeping**: A single platform to manage exposure values across different locations.
- **Automation and Accuracy**: Automate exposure collection and reporting processes, ensuring consistency and accuracy.
- **Future Value Prediction**: Project future exposure values using historical data and trends.
- **Renewal Planning**: Adjust inputs to support renewal planning and evolving risk strategies.

### Risk Tracking and Management
- **Construction and Occupancy**: Collect and report on Construction, Occupancy, Protection, and Exposure data from across the organization.
- **State/Job Classification**: Track exposures by state and job class for workers' compensation and other insurance programs.
- **Payroll Integration**: Reduce manual entry and improve accuracy using payroll systems.

### Incident and Event Management
- **Incident Collection**: Capture accurate incident data using flexible intake tools.
- **Workflow Automation**: Automate workflows and TPA feeds for efficient incident management.
- **Root Cause Analysis**: Analyze root causes to understand and prevent future occurrences.

### Policy and Claim Management
- **Policy Administration**: Centralize insurance information and connect it to claims data.
- **Claims Processing**: Consolidate claims data across all lines of coverage to streamline workflows.
- **Trend Identification**: Identify trends and reduce litigation costs.
- **Claim Outcomes**: Improve claims outcomes through proactive risk management.

### Risk Allocation
- **Cost Precision**: Allocate risk management costs across the organization based on configurable formulaic models.
- **Flexible Cost Element Selection**: Select appropriate cost elements for allocation.
- **Integration with TCOR Analytics**: Seamless integration with TCOR Analytics for dynamic AI-powered calculations.
