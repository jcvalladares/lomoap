# Arnold Clark: A Case Study

## Summary
Arnold Clark, a UK's largest independently owned car retailer, implemented Origami Risk's platform to enhance its risk and compliance management. The platform provided streamlined workflows, centralized data management, and flexible configuration to handle diverse business requirements. By adopting Origami Risk, Arnold Clark achieved significant operational improvements, including streamlined compliance auditing, enhanced visibility, and improved communication with third-party suppliers. The platform's adaptability and responsive support model further strengthened the organisation's ability to meet its evolving needs.

### Key Points
1. **Centralised Data Management**: Arnold Clark managed insurable risk data centrally, improving overall visibility and efficiency.
2. **Streamlined Workflow**: The platform streamlined various business processes, including compliance inspections, maintenance activities, and major construction projects.
3. **Flexible Configuration**: The platform allowed for tailored solutions across different department needs.
4. **Adaptive Technology**: Origami Risk's adaptable nature ensured flexibility in meeting changing business requirements.
5. **Responsiveness**: The platform's responsive support model helped in addressing unusual requests and maintaining a positive relationship with the organisation.

### Impact
Origami Risk's platform significantly improved Arnold Clark's risk and compliance management, leading to operational enhancements and better decision-making. The platform's focus on flexibility and adaptability made it a valuable addition to the organisation's risk and insurance ecosystem.
