# Origami Risk Leadership Team

## Meet the Team Driving Origami Risk Forward with Business and Tech Innovation Insights

### Earne Bentley, Chief Revenue Officer

**Bio:** Earne Bentley is an accomplished risk management technology executive who has successfully led various sales and service practices throughout his career. Since 2019, he has held the position of President, Risk Solutions at Origami Risk. In this capacity, Earne spearheads the strategic direction of the development, sales, and service for a range of products including RMIS, GRC, EHS, and Healthcare solutions. His responsibilities also extend to ensuring Origami's values and practices are brought to life for the company's clients and employees. Additionally, he actively seeks and guides opportunities for expansion and growth through the development of new business relationships. Earne is the recipient of a Business Insurance Breakout Award and an alumnus of Auburn University.

### Ryan Cantor, Chief Product & Technology Officer

**Bio:** As Chief Product and Technology Officer at Origami Risk, Ryan Cantor leads the global Product and Technology organization, spanning product management, engineering, UI/UX, cloud operations, and internal IT. With a team of over 300 professionals, he drives innovation and execution across Origami’s high-growth, cloud-based SaaS platform serving the Risk Management and Core Insurance markets. Ryan brings over 15 years of executive leadership experience in SaaS, product strategy, and technology. Prior to joining Origami Risk, he served as Chief Product Officer at Thryv, where he led both product and engineering teams from inception to over $300M in annual recurring revenue. Under his leadership, Thryv delivered solutions to more than 85,000 small and mid-sized businesses across the U.S., Canada, Australia, and New Zealand, achieving 1,400% growth in user engagement and consistently delivering 25%+ annual revenue growth—profitably. Known for his pragmatic, data-driven leadership style, Ryan emphasizes vision, culture, and accountability. He champions a high-performing, collaborative environment that empowers teams to focus, execute, and deliver business-critical results. As a coach and mentor, he is committed to developing talent and fostering organizational resilience. Ryan is recognized for his ability to simplify complexity, lead through change, and keep teams aligned amid ambiguity.
