## Mobile App for On-Site Risk and Safety Data

### Summary
Origami Mobile is a comprehensive platform designed to streamline on-site risk and safety data collection and analysis. It offers an intuitive app for on-the-go reporting, seamless API access, expert system configuration, customizable solutions, and industry-specific solutions. The platform enhances operations with speed, scalability, and security, providing a single source of truth for data management and work streams.

### Key Features

#### Mobile App
- **Real-time Data Collection**: Capture field data instantly and accurately.
- **Secure Sign-in**: Easy access with credentials, shared codes, or biometric authentication.
- **Offline Access**: Complete tasks without internet connectivity.
- **Smart Navigation**: Identify key information quickly with form search and favorites.
- **Automatic Timeout**: Prevent unauthorized access when devices are left unattended.

#### API Access
- **Integration**: Seamless integration with data sources.
- **Automation Tools**: Boost efficiency with automation tools.

#### Reporting and Analytics
- **Configurable Dashboards**: Customizable dashboards for risk, safety, and insurance.
- **Pre-Built Reports**: Pre-built and configurable reports for decision-making.

#### Resource Management
- **Asset Tracking**: Manage assets, tasks, people, and sites.
- **Document Management**: Securely store and generate documents and PDFs.
