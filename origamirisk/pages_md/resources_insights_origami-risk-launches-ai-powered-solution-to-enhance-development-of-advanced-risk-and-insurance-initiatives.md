### Title: Origami Risk Launches AI-Powered Solution to Enhance Development of Advanced Risk and Insurance Initiatives

#### Summary:
Origami Risk, the industry-leading risk, safety, and insurance software as a service (SaaS) technology firm, today announced the launch of a new solution driven by artificial intelligence (AI). This AI-powered solution allows risk managers to quickly create or expand risk registers for governance, risk and compliance (GRC) and enterprise risk management (ERM) initiatives. The new AI Risk and Control Explorer automatically generates lists of enterprise exposures based on inputs of key information such as industry, employee count, and geographic location, enhancing the ability of risk management teams to build or scale ERM and compliance programs, as well as to design and implement internal controls. This not only further protects their organizations, but does so in a fraction of the time it would have taken otherwise.

#### Key Features:
- **AI-driven Risk Register Creation**: Automatically generates lists of enterprise exposures.
- **Real-time Monitoring**: Provides insights into real-time risk and compliance issues.
- **Flexible Customization**: Allows for customization of risk registers and controls.
- **Secure Platform**: Built on a secure, scalable platform.
- **User-Friendly Interface**: Intuitive app for on-the-go reporting.
- **Integration with Data Sources**: Seamless integration with various data sources.

#### Additional Highlights:
- **Future-Looking Projections**: Predicts the impact of potential investments on total cost of risk (TCOR).
- **Flexible Analytics**: Tracks trends and measures performance against internal standards.
- **Generative AI Text Assistance**: Helps employers save time and accelerate claims and incident management communication through email with intelligent text recommendations.
