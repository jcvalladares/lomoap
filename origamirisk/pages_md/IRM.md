# Integrated Risk Management (IRM)

## Overview

Origami Risk offers a comprehensive suite of integrated solutions for risk, safety, and compliance, designed to enhance operations and drive better business decisions. Our platform centralizes risk management data, automates processes, and provides real-time insights to empower leadership and drive continuous improvement.

### Key Features

1. **Risk Management Information System (RMIS)**:
   - Centralizes insurable risk data.
   - Streamlines communications and reduces admin tasks.

2. **Environment, Health and Safety (EHS)**:
   - Ensures consistent safety standards and training.
   - Automates safety data collection and analysis.

3. **Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC)**:
   - Maintains policies and tracks risks.
   - Ensures compliance and reduces exposure.

4. **Healthcare**:
   - Integrates risk, safety, and compliance on one platform.
   - Supports healthcare providers and organizations.

5. **Construction**:
   - Manages risk and safety on site and off.
   - Provides scalable tools for managing complex projects.

6. **Government Risk and Safety**:
   - Offers solutions for public entities and government agencies.

7. **Manufacturing**:
   - Ensures resilient operations and minimizes risk exposure.

8. **Transportation**:
   - Helps businesses navigate the complexities of transportation risks.

### Benefits

- **Streamlined Operations**: Centralized data helps streamline daily tasks and reduce manual work.
- **Real-Time Insights**: Automated workflows and dashboards provide real-time monitoring and decision-making capabilities.
- **Collaboration**: Seamless integration with data sources allows for easy collaboration among departments.
- **Data Quality**: Flexible intake of structured data ensures reliable insights.
- **Scalability**: Configurable tools and modular design facilitate adaptation to changing needs.
- **Security**: Role-based access, audit trails, and compliance certifications ensure robust protection of sensitive data.

### Integrations

Our platform integrates with various data sources, allowing seamless data flow across different departments. This integration enhances collaboration and drives informed decision-making.
