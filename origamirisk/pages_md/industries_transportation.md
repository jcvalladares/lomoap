## Transportation and Logistics Risk and Safety Software

### Overview
Origami Risk provides comprehensive risk and safety software designed for businesses on the move. It offers a range of solutions tailored to streamline processes, enhance safety culture, and ensure regulatory compliance.

### Key Features
1. **Smart Drivers**: Identify trends to safeguard workers with accident and near-miss details captured via mobile app, telematics, and more.
2. **Control Costs**: Automate workflows and centralize data for analysis to inform decisions.
3. **Stay Compliant**: Drive DOT, OSHA, and FMCSA compliance with tools to document driver qualifications and track safety metrics.
4. **Integrated Risk Management**: One platform for total risk and safety visibility.
5. **Environment, Health and Safety (EHS)**: Support a stronger safety culture by understanding and proactively addressing the causes of accidents and injuries using on-the-go event reporting, mobile audits, and intuitive analytics.
6. **Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC)**: Strengthen compliance and operational resilience through enterprise risk management, business continuity, and vendor risk management programs.
7. **Healthcare**: Integrated solutions for healthcare providers.
8. **Industries**: Empower your organization with solutions that streamline operations and turn data into actionable insights.
