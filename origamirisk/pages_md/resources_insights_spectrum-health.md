## Spectrum Health's EHS Solution: How Origami Risk Helped Overcome Challenges

### Summary
Spectrum Health, an integrated health system in Southwest and West Michigan, faced numerous challenges related to incident management, stakeholder communication, and overall safety culture. Origami Risk's EHS solution provided a comprehensive solution that addressed these issues, leading to improved visibility, stakeholder engagement, and enhanced operational efficiency. The solution enabled seamless access to injury reporting forms, real-time sharing of information, and consistent reporting across the organization.

### Key Features and Benefits
1. **Single Sign-On Accessibility**: Enhanced collaboration among team members.
2. **Automated Sharing of Details**: Automatically shared with relevant stakeholders.
3. **Holistic Dashboard**: Facilitated tracking of incidents and trends.
4. **Statistical Analysis**: Provided insights into safety performance.
5. **Marketing Automation**: Created targeted campaigns for stakeholders.
6. **User Profiles**: Enabled personalized communication and resource allocation.

### Case Study
**Case Study:** Spectrum Health's Safety Pause Program

**Challenges:**
- Inadequate paper-based incident tracking.
- Difficulty in sharing and accessing incident details.
- Limited visibility of safety events across different departments.

**Solution:**
- Single sign-on access for all team members.
- Automatic sharing of incident details.
- Comprehensive dashboard for tracking incidents and trends.

**Outcome:**
- Greater visibility of all safety events.
- Detailed data on injuries and exposures.
- Improved coordination among departments.
- Enhanced stakeholder engagement.

### Conclusion
Origami Risk's EHS solution effectively addressed Spectrum Health's safety challenges, improving incident management, stakeholder communication, and overall operational efficiency. By leveraging technology, the health system was able to transform its approach to safety and improve its culture of safety. This case study demonstrates how Origami Risk can help organizations overcome similar challenges and achieve sustainable improvements in safety performance.
