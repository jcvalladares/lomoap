## DHL's Experience with Origami Risk RMIS

### Overview
DHL, a leading global delivery and logistics company, has implemented the Origami Risk RMIS to enhance its risk management capabilities in the UK and Ireland. The RMIS provides speed, flexibility, automation, data import, and export tools, claims management tools, analysis, and reporting tools, among others.

### Key Benefits
- **Real-Time Access**: DHL can now access loss data and claim trends in real-time.
- **Efficiency**: The system accelerates the processing and resolution of individual claims.
- **Cost Management**: Loss costs are allocated to specific cost centers, promoting safety and loss prevention.
- **Data Integration**: All insurance policy and coverage information is managed centrally.
- **Safety Culture**: DHL’s risk management team’s strong safety culture has enabled the implementation of the RMIS.
- **Scalability**: The system supports growth across multiple locations and countries.

### Implementation Process
DHL began implementing the Origami Risk RMIS in 2015 and is currently expanding its implementation to other areas. The pilot accident reporting program with Origami Risk has shown promising results, and the company plans to continue expanding the implementation to additional countries.

### Impact
The implementation of the Origami Risk RMIS has led to increased efficiency in DHL’s cargo claims, improved safety and performance, and enhanced customer satisfaction. The company has received awards for its implementation, demonstrating the effectiveness of the technology in driving positive change.

### Conclusion
DHL's successful implementation of the Origami Risk RMIS has revolutionized its risk management processes, making it easier to handle claims, monitor risks, and optimize operations. The system's flexibility and scalability make it a valuable tool for organizations looking to improve their risk management capabilities.
