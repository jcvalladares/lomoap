## P&C Insurance: Workers' Compensation Solutions

### Overview
P&C Insurance offers comprehensive workers' compensation solutions designed to streamline claim administration, improve compliance, and enhance outcomes. Their platform includes AI tools, seamless integration with data sources, expert system configuration and guidance, mobile reporting and analytics, purpose-built solutions, and cloud-native tools for policy, billing, and claims. The company's commitment to innovation and customer-centric approach ensures that their products meet the needs of both insurers and employers.
