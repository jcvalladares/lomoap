## Incident & Events Management Software

### Overview
Origami Risk's Incident & Event Management software provides a comprehensive solution to capture, report, and manage incidents. It offers mobile tools, centralized data, and automated workflows to enhance efficiency and drive immediate action. By leveraging advanced AI tools and seamless integration with HR and payroll systems, Origami Risk helps improve data accuracy, reduce delays, and drive proactive loss control and safety strategies.

### Key Features
1. **Mobile Tools**: Employees can report incidents instantaneously through mobile devices.
2. **Centralized Data**: All incident data is centrally managed for easy access and analysis.
3. **Automated Workflows**: Automated workflows streamline investigations and corrective actions, enhancing accountability and reducing recurrence.
4. **Triage and Transmission**: Incident data is securely transmitted to TPAs and carriers for accelerated claims processes.
5. **Integrated Analytics**: Comprehensive analytics uncover trends, measure corrective action impact, and benchmark performance across locations.

### Solutions
- **Risk Management Information Systems (RMIS)**: Centralizes insurable risk data for streamlined management.
- **TCOR Analytics**: Measures risk investment impact, accelerates claims resolution, and unifies policy information.
- **Insurance Program Management**: Unifies policy information for analysis and renewal activities.
- **Allocations**: Allocates costs with precision based on configurable models.
- **Exposure Management**: Tracks exposures efficiently and forecasts exposure changes.
