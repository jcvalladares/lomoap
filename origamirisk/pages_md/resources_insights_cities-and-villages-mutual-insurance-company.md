### Cities and Villages Mutual Insurance Company (CVMIC) RMIS Case Study

#### Problem Statement
CVMIC faced challenges with an outdated on-premises system that limited accessibility and collaboration for both staff and members. This case study explores how CVMIC leveraged Origami Risk's cloud-based platform to enhance member engagement and streamline operations.

#### Solution
CVMIC implemented a customized member portal and automating key processes. The solution enabled members to report incidents and handle claims themselves, while reducing staff workload. The platform also centralized and managed insurable risk data, providing a comprehensive view of the organization's risks.

#### Impact
The implementation resulted in significant improvements in incident reporting, claims handling, and data accessibility. Members were able to access their own information quickly and easily, leading to increased trust and satisfaction. Staff workload was significantly reduced, allowing CVMIC to focus on other critical tasks.

#### Conclusion
CVMIC's adoption of Origami Risk's cloud-based platform successfully addressed its challenges and improved its operational efficiency. The platform provided a seamless experience for both members and staff, resulting in enhanced risk management and improved customer satisfaction.
