### Washington Schools Risk Management Pool (WSRMP) Case Study

#### Overview
The Washington Schools Risk Management Pool (WSRMP) faced significant challenges with its outdated and inefficient risk information management system. The pool's legacy system was unscalable, inefficient, and prone to errors, leading to delays in member services and poor decision-making. WSRMP recognized the need for a top-notch solution and selected Origami Risk's comprehensive pool management information system (PMIS).

#### Solution Implementation
Origami Risk provided the pool with a streamlined platform that addressed the key issues it faced. The system allowed for accurate recovery calculations, enhanced reporting, and improved incident tracking. The online risk management portal enabled instant reporting and incident management, reducing paperwork and improving response times.

#### Key Features and Benefits
1. **Accurate Recovery Calculations**: Origami ensured that all recoveries were calculated correctly, reducing the likelihood of errors.
2. **Real-Time Reporting**: The platform offered real-time reporting, allowing for timely intervention and analysis of trends.
3. **Efficient Workflow**: Automating payment processing and claims settlement reduced the time spent on manual tasks and increased efficiency.
4. **Data-Driven Report Cards**: WSRMP established data-driven report cards to monitor and improve risk management.
5. **Continuous Improvement**: WSRMP continued to collaborate with Origami on new solutions to enhance its capabilities.

#### Impact on Members
By adopting Origami Risk, WSRMP improved member satisfaction and operational efficiency. The pool was able to better serve and retain its members and improve their risk management and insurance services.

#### Lessons Learned
The case study highlights the importance of leveraging modern technology to address inefficiencies and improve operations. It underscores the value of collaboration between the pool and technology providers to achieve successful outcomes.
