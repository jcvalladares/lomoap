# EHS Audits, Inspections & Observations Solution Sheet

## Overview
The EHS Audits, Inspections & Observations Solution Sheet provides a comprehensive framework for conducting equipment, asset, building, and process audits using the Web Browser or Origami Mobile app. This solution aims to empower safety professionals and workers to proactively identify risks and hazards, preventing incidents and injuries before they occur.

## Features
1. **Real-time Data Collection**: Access audit results directly from your web browser or mobile app.
2. **Intuitive Interface**: Easy navigation through various sections to perform audits efficiently.
3. **Data Integration**: Seamlessly integrate with your existing data sources for accurate audit data.
4. **Expert Guidance**: Expert system configuration and guidance to ensure efficient and effective audits.
5. **On-the-Go Reporting**: Intuitive app for on-the-go reporting, allowing users to quickly generate reports.
6. **Configurable Dashboards**: Customizable dashboards for easy monitoring and analysis of audit results.
7. **Purpose-Built Solutions**: Tailored solutions for risk, safety, and insurance, enhancing operational efficiency.
