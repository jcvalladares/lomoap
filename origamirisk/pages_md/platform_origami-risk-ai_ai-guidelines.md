# AI Guidelines for Resilient Business Operations

## Overview
Origami Risk is dedicated to designing AI that is transparent, accessible, and delivers real-world results. Our approach is rooted in privacy, responsibility, and high-quality, ensuring that clients stay in control every step of the way.

### Key Principles
1. **Privacy First**: AI tools are designed to protect your data with confidence.
2. **Responsible and Ethical**: Trust is earned through transparency. We give clients visibility into how AI works and the flexibility to shape AI workflows to their organization's needs.
3. **High Quality**: High-impact results with real-world tested tools.

### Purpose-built Tools
- **Real Client Needs**: Human oversight for critical workflows.
- **Continuous Testing and Refinement**: Ongoing oversight and accountability.
- **AI Technology Evolution**: Maintaining responsible AI practices through a structured, stakeholder-driven review process.
