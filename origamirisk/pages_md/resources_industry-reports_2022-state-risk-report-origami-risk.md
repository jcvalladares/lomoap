### 2024 State of Risk Report

#### Summary
The 2024 State of Risk report provides insights into the evolving nature of the risk environment, focusing on the challenges faced by risk professionals across various industries. Key trends include balancing traditional risk management with emerging technologies, understanding familiar elements in emerging risks, and preparing for the future with actionable strategies.

#### Headings
- **Key Trends**
  - **Dynamic Risk Environment**: Emphasizes the need for balance in managing risk.
  - **Novel Risks**: Identifies familiar elements within emerging risks.
  - **Preparedness**: Emphasizes the importance of proactive preparation.
  
- **Recommendations**
  - **Balancing Traditional and Emerging Risks**: Provides practical advice on managing both types of risks.
  - **Identifying Familiar Elements**: Offers tips for recognizing familiar elements in emerging risks.
  - **Preparing for the Future**: Recommends actionable strategies for preparing for the future.

#### Content
- **Survey Results**: Highlights the diverse roles of risk professionals and their current positions.
- **Trends Addressed**: Includes statistics on the prevalence of emerging risks and the need for balanced risk management.
- **Recommendations**: Offers practical advice on preparing for the future and managing risk effectively.
- **Case Studies**: Shares real-world success stories from Origami Risk clients.
- **Industry Reports**: Provides insights into the latest developments in the risk management sector.

This report aims to help organizations navigate the changing landscape of risk management and improve their ability to respond to evolving threats.
