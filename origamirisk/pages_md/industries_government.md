# Risk & Safety Solutions for Government Entities

## Overview

Origami Risk offers a suite of solutions designed to enhance government entity operations through speed, scalability, and security. Our platform provides AI-powered tools for risk assessment, seamless integration with data sources, expert system configuration and guidance, and an intuitive mobile app for on-the-go reporting. Our solutions are purpose-built for risk, safety, and insurance, and can be tailored to meet the needs of various industries.
