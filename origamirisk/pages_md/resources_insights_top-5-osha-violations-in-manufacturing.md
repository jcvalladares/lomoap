# Top 5 OSHA Violations in Manufacturing

## Overview
In 2024, there were more than 16,000 OSHA violations in the manufacturing sector, resulting in penalties exceeding $70 million. The most frequently cited violations were Control of hazardous energies (lockout/tagout), Respiratory Protection, Powered industrial trucks (forklifts), General requirements for all machines (machine guarding), and Hazard Communication. By investing in the right technology, organizations can prevent future violations and protect worker productivity and well-being.

## Recommendations
- **Implement Digital Job Safety Analyses (JSAs) and Asset-Specific Audits**: For example, attaching QR codes to individual machines allows workers to quickly access and complete relevant safety checklists via mobile devices.

- **Invest in Technology to Build a Strong Safety Culture**: Building a strong safety culture is about far more than avoiding OSHA violations — it’s making sure your employees go home safe.
