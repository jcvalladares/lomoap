# Larimer County Risk Management Platform

## Overview
Larimer County, a seventh-largest county in Colorado, uses an innovative platform called Origami Risk to enhance operations and manage risk effectively. The platform includes AI tools, seamless integration with data sources, expert system configuration and guidance, and customizable dashboards for reporting and analytics. The department serves as a central partner to all 26 operational groups, ensuring transparency and proactive risk management.

## Key Features
- **Risk Management Information Systems (RMIS)**: Configurable reports and dashboards for analyzing trends and identifying patterns.
- **API Access**: Seamless integration with various data sources.
- **Implementation and Support**: Expert system configuration and guidance.
- **Reporting and Analytics Solutions**: Configurable dashboards for reporting and analytics.
- **Purpose-built Solutions**: Improving outcomes with configurable, integrated solutions for risk, safety, and insurance.
- **Environment, Health & Safety (EHS)**: Operational Risk Management for a strong safety culture.
- **Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)**: Identifying, assessing, and mitigating enterprise risk.
- **Business Continuity Management**: Enterprise Risk Management and internal audit.
- **Third-Party Risk Management**: Managing compliance within a single platform.
- **Healthcare Solutions**: Integrated solutions for healthcare providers.
- **Industries**: Empowering organizations to streamline operations and turn data into actionable insights.
- **Resource Library**: Discover new perspectives and practical insights.
- **Case Studies**: Real-world success stories.
- **Events**: Connect, learn, and network.

## Success Stories
- Reduced workers' compensation claims and lower costs in over fifteen-year span despite 30% employee growth.
- Improved ergonomics and reduced workplace injuries.
- Enhanced compliance with DOT regulations and ADA Title II compliance.
- Measurable outcomes demonstrating mission success.

## Impact
The platform has significantly improved risk management practices at Larimer County, leading to better resource protection and cost savings. It has been recognized by PRIMA with the 2025 Public Risk Manager of the Year Award and has been serving as the technology foundation supporting the department's risk management program.

## Future Prospects
Larimer County continues to leverage Origami Risk to further improve its risk management practices and ensure the safety and well-being of its residents.
