# Origami Risk Profiled in Celent's 2025 Policy Administration Systems Vendors Report

## Summary
Celent's Policy Administration Systems Vendors: North American P&C Insurance 2025 report highlights Origami Risk's capabilities in the insurance sector, emphasizing its robust features for loss control, premium audit, and workers' compensation claims. The report also notes that Origami's core system is highly configurable and scalable, making it suitable for large-scale insurance operations.

## Key Features
- **User Interface**: Clean and customizable, offering features to make user tasks faster, easier, and more convenient.
- **Pricing Calculations**: Ready access to pricing calculations by clicking "through to view rating worksheets" and dynamic updates based on rating factors.
- **Role-Based Access Control**: Robust features for user groups and roles that allow for permissioning down to the user level.
- **Integration**: Seamless integration with various data sources, enhancing operational efficiency.

## Vendor Recognition
Celent recognizes Origami Risk as a Functional Standout in their Technical Capability Matrix for policy administration systems. The report also praises Origami's features for loss control, premium audit, and workers' compensation claims, highlighting its strong position in the industry.

## Additional Information
Origami Risk offers a full suite of end-to-end, integrated SaaS solutions for policy, rating, billing, loss control and claims administration, reporting and analytics, along with a full-service bureau content management solution and a comprehensive digital engagement experience for all internal and external stakeholders. This comprehensive approach ensures seamless operation and actionable insights for insurers.
