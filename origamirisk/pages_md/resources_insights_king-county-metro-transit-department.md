# King County Metro Transit Department's Transformation with Origami Risk

## Summary
King County Metro, one of the largest public transit systems in the United States, has transformed its safety program using Origami Risk's comprehensive EHS suite. The agency centralized critical safety initiatives data and processes, reducing manual data entry and time-consuming tasks for safety team members and drivers' supervisors. The implementation of Origami Risk has led to increased efficiency, greater acceptance of technology, and solidified a strong safety culture among the organization.
