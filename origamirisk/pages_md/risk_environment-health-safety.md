## Environment, Health & Safety (EHS) Software

### Overview
Origami Risk is an innovative platform designed to enhance operations with speed, scalability, and security. It provides a range of AI tools, seamless integration with data sources, expert system configuration and guidance, and intuitive apps for on-the-go reporting. Our solutions are purpose-built for risk, safety, and insurance, offering configurable, integrated solutions that improve outcomes and drive better business decisions.

### Features
- **Comprehensive Solutions**: Centralizes safety insights, automates reporting, and supports proactive safety management.
- **Operational Risk Management**: Manages operational risk, including documents and workflows related to JSA, LOTO, permit to work, and more.
- **Environmental Compliance**: Ensures regulatory compliance and minimizes hazardous material tracking.
- **Occupational Health**: Protects employees and the environment with tools to minimize exposure risks.
- **Case Studies**: Showcase real-world success stories from our clients.
- **News and Insights**: Stay informed about latest trends and best practices.

### Industries
Our software is tailored for various industries, including P&C Insurance, Construction, Government, Healthcare, Manufacturing, and Transportation. It helps streamline operations and turn data into actionable insights, promoting a culture of safety.
