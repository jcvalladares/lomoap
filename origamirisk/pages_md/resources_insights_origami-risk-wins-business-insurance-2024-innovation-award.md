### Title: Origami Risk Wins Business Insurance 2024 Innovation Award

#### Summary:
Origami Risk, the industry-leading risk, safety, and insurance software as a service (SaaS) firm, has won the 2024 Innovation Award from Business Insurance magazine for its Origami Mobile solution. The company has been recognized for its continuous innovation in product design and delivery.

#### Key Features:
- **Real-time Visibility**: Allows risk and safety professionals to monitor workplace incidents, remote job-site risks, and hazards even in areas with limited or no internet connection.
- **Intuitive App**: Provides easy access to data collected from the field using Origami Risk's web app, enabling proactive control and prevention of incidents.
- **Centralized Data Management**: Ensures comprehensive tracking and management of insurable risk data across different departments.
- **Automated Workflows**: Streamlines critical processes such as policy administration, billing, and claims.
- **Collaborative Culture**: Fosters a collaborative environment where clients and employees work together to achieve common goals.

#### Impact:
The award underscores Origami Risk's commitment to delivering high-quality solutions that enhance risk management and safety performance. It also highlights the company's ability to adapt to changing market demands and customer needs through continuous innovation.

#### Next Steps:
The company plans to continue celebrating its achievements with events and workshops to share knowledge and best practices among risk management professionals. Additionally, it aims to expand its offerings to cater to a wider range of industries, including construction, government, healthcare, manufacturing, and transportation.
