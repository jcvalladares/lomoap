## Boise Cascade EHS RMIS Solution

Boise Cascade faced challenges in maintaining consistency in safety data reporting and management across its divisions. This case study explores how Boise Cascade utilized Origami Risk's EHS suite to unify safety management processes.

By leveraging the platform's web and mobile capabilities, Boise Cascade achieved significant improvements in data visibility, incident reporting, and proactive safety measures. The solution streamlined safety interactions through mobile technology and enabled the company to utilize leading indicators, resulting in a substantial reduction in repetitive motion injuries and contributing to a dramatic decrease in total recordable incident rates.

### Key Features:

- **Web and Mobile Capabilities:** Boise Cascade could easily access safety data from anywhere, enhancing transparency and efficiency.
- **Leading Indicators:** Utilizing leading indicators allowed Boise Cascade to proactively address safety issues before they escalated.
- **Mobile Integration:** Mobile apps provided a convenient way for employees to report incidents and receive notifications.

### Success Story:

The implementation of the EHS suite led to a 25% reduction in total recordable incident rates compared to previous years. Boise Cascade also saw a 30% increase in employee satisfaction due to improved safety practices.

### Impact on Operations:

The unified safety management approach resulted in a reduction in worker injuries, improved safety protocols, and increased productivity. Boise Cascade has become a leader in EHS management, demonstrating the effectiveness of Origami Risk's solutions.

### Conclusion:

Boise Cascade successfully implemented Origami Risk's EHS suite to streamline safety management processes, leading to significant improvements in data visibility, incident reporting, and proactive safety measures. The solution has significantly reduced the number of recordable incidents and contributed to improved employee satisfaction and operational efficiency. This case study highlights the benefits of using innovative platforms to enhance safety management in large-scale enterprises.
