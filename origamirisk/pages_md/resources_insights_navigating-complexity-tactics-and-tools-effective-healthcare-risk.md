### Navigating Complexity: Tactics and Tools for Effective Healthcare Risk Management

#### Summary
Join presenters Taylor Potoczak, CPHRM, Director of Enterprise Risk & Insurance at Cleveland Clinic, and Anooja Cannon, MS, CPHQ, Senior Market Strategy Lead at Origami Risk, to explore how a holistic approach supported by robust tools can transform healthcare risk management practices by improving collaboration, streamlining daily workflows, and providing access to data that informs decision-making. This webinar provides insights from Cleveland Clinic and Origami's collaborative journey, highlighting how technology can empower risk management teams to achieve greater efficiency and transparency, allowing them to focus on what truly matters.

#### Key Points
- **Holistic Approach**: A comprehensive solution that integrates multiple components.
- **Robust Tools**: Advanced AI and APIs for streamlined processes.
- **Collaboration**: Encouraging teamwork among departments.
- **Data Accessibility**: Ensuring teams have access to relevant information.
- **Efficiencies**: Reducing manual tasks and increasing productivity.
- **Transparency**: Providing real-time insights and decision-making capabilities.
