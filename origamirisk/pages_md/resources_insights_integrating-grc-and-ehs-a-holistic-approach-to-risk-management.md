### Integrating GRC and EHS: A Holistic Approach to Risk Management

#### Summary
Origami Risk offers a comprehensive solution that seamlessly integrates Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) and Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) systems. By connecting these domains, organizations can achieve a more holistic approach to risk management, driving smarter, more aligned decision-making across the board. Key benefits include enhanced visibility, streamlined workflows, and proactive risk management practices. Whether in manufacturing, government, transportation, construction, or healthcare, the need for integrated risk management is universal.

#### Main Features
1. **Seamless Integration**: Combines EHS and GRC systems to provide a unified view of risk.
2. **Real-Time Visibility**: Enables executives and ERM teams to gain clear understanding of safety risks.
3. **Automated Workflows**: Reduces administrative burden and improves decision-making.
4. **Standardized Scoring Frameworks**: Ensures consistent prioritization across departments.
5. **Gamified Benchmarking**: Encourages engagement at the site level.
6. **Data-driven Decision Making**: Enables informed risk management and strategic planning.

#### Benefits Across Verticals
- **Manufacturing**: Identifies high-risk processes and justifies investments in automation or PPE.
- **Government**: Streamlines compliance tracking across departments and improves transparency.
- **Healthcare**: Links safety initiatives to patient outcomes and regulatory performance.
- **Construction**: Benchmarks safety performance across job sites and reduces incident rates.
- **Transportation**: Aligns safety data with business continuity planning and fleet management.
