### Title: GRC Software for Enterprise Risk & Compliance

#### Summary:
Origami Risk offers a comprehensive suite of tools and platforms designed to enhance enterprise risk management, business continuity, and vendor risk management. With AI-powered analytics and customizable solutions, Origami Risk helps organizations proactively identify and mitigate risks, leading to improved decision-making and operational resilience.

#### Features:
- **Enterprise Risk Management (ERM)**: Identifies, assesses, manages, and monitors organizational risks.
- **Business Continuity Management**: Increases operational resilience by building and managing continuity plans.
- **Third-Party Risk Management**: Mitigates vendor and contractor liability.
- **Policy & Procedure Management**: Develops, communicates, and maintains corporate policies and procedures.
- **Internal Controls Management**: Understands internal risk control effectiveness and tests and manages controls.
- **Compliance Management**: Measures and reports on organizational compliance.

#### Case Studies:
- **Panduit Scales ERM Program**: Reduces administrative burden and enhances risk engagement.
- **McCarthy Building Companies**: Automates and streamlines risk management through its Mitigation Action Planning (MAP) program.

#### Technologies:
- **Cookies**: Used for storing and accessing device information.
- **Analytics**: AI-powered tools for insight and automation.
- **User Profiles**: Created for sending advertising and tracking user behavior.

#### Legal Considerations:
- **Privacy**: Collects and stores data only for legitimate purposes.
- **Consent**: Requires explicit consent for processing data.
- **Trust Center**: Manages consent and privacy settings.
