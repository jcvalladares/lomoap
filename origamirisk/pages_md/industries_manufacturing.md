# Risk Management Software for Modern Manufacturing

## Overview
Origami Risk is a comprehensive risk management software designed specifically for modern manufacturing. It offers a range of features to enhance operations, improve safety, and reduce overall risk.

## Key Features
- **AI Tools**: Boost speed, insight, and impact with AI-powered tools.
- **API Access**: Seamless integration with your data sources.
- **Implementation and Support**: Expert system configuration and guidance.
- **Mobile App**: Intuitive app for on-the-go reporting.
- **Reporting and Analytics**: Configurable dashboards.
- **Purpose-built Solutions**: Improve outcomes with customizable, integrated solutions for risk, safety, and insurance.
- **Industry-Specific Solutions**: Built for various industries such as P&C Insurance, Construction, Government, Healthcare, Manufacturing, and Transportation.
