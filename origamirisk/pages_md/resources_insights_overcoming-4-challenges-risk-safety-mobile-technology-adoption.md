# Overcoming 4 Challenges to Risk & Safety Mobile Technology Adoption

## Summary
Risk and safety mobile technology adoption presents numerous challenges, including negative user perception, hardware investment, inclusivity, and user comprehension. However, with proper planning and execution, these challenges can be overcome to ensure effective implementation and maximize the benefits of mobile technology in improving risk and safety management.

## Key Points
1. **User Perception**: Ensure a user-friendly interface and simple navigation.
2. **IT Department**: Collaborate with IT to understand and address technical issues.
3. **Inclusivity**: Provide accessibility features and support for non-native speakers and people with disabilities.
4. **Hardware Investment**: Consider alternative deployment methods like kiosks or limited device distribution.
5. **Feedback Loop**: Establish regular communication and feedback mechanisms to maintain motivation and engagement.

## Best Practices
1. **Engage End Users**: Involve users in the development and testing process.
2. **Train Employees**: Provide comprehensive training to ensure seamless use.
3. **Implement Incentives**: Offer rewards for high-performing users.
4. **Maintain Feedback Loop**: Regularly update and communicate outcomes to motivate continued use.
