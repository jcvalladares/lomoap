## Title: Building TRUST in AI: A Framework for Risk & Insurance Leaders

### Summary:
Origami's TRUST framework is a comprehensive approach to integrating artificial intelligence (AI) into risk-sensitive environments, ensuring that AI strengthens operations while mitigating risks. The framework emphasizes principles of tactical anchor, responsibility, user control, security, and transparency, providing practical steps and guidelines for successful AI implementation. The book "The AI Maturity Roadmap for Risk and Insurance Leaders" offers detailed insights and checklists to help organizations navigate the complexities of AI adoption in the risk management space.
