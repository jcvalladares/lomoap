## Overview
Origami Risk offers a comprehensive suite of third-party administrators designed to streamline operations and exceed client expectations. Our solutions are purpose-built for risk, safety, and insurance, providing configurable, integrated solutions that improve outcomes and support long-term growth.

## Key Features
### Claims Administration
- **Risk Management Information System (RMIS)**: Optimizes claims adjudication, reduces loss ratios, and delivers exceptional service.
- **Automated Workflows**: Reduces claim cycle times and improves resolution.
- **Embedded Tools**: Ensures compliance and transparency through streamlined reporting and subrogation tracking.

### Infrastructure
- **Cloud-native solutions**: Flexibility to power any line of business.
- **Commercial Lines**: Streamlines claims operations for carriers, MGAs, and businesses across industries.
- **Workers’ Compensation**: Simplifies case management and drives efficiency.
- **Medical Professional Liability (MPL)**: Connects with data from hospitals, counsel, and reinsurers to manage and defend MPL claims more efficiently.
- **Personal Lines**: Empowers adjusters and clients with seamless digital experiences.

### Data Management
- **Secure Access**: Offers secure access to data, dashboards, reports, and more via a branded portal.
- **Self-service Configuration**: Onboard new clients, adjust rules, and manage reporting—on your schedule.

### Compliance and Security
- **Automated Routing**: Supports adjusters' decisions based on predefined rules.
- **Data Governance**: Ensures compliance with regulations and maintains data integrity.

### Additional Services
- **Marketing**: Creates user profiles for sending advertising and tracks user engagement.
- **Manage Options**: Allows users to manage their subscriptions, services, and preferences.
