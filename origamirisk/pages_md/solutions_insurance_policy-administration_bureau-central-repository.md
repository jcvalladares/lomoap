# P&C Policy Administration | Bureau Central Repository (BCR)

## Overview

The P&C Policy Administration | Bureau Central Repository (BCR) provides a streamlined platform for accelerating bureau updates, simplifying content adoption, and freeing teams to focus on growth without maintenance. It offers solutions that optimize bureau content management, eliminate repetitive configuration, and respond faster to market changes. Additionally, it supports bureau coverage across major lines of business and delivers updates via scheduled migrations and controls that preserve business-specific deviations.

## Key Features

### Bureau Content Update Efficiency and Accuracy

- **Bureau Circular Expertise**: Stay up to date with bureau circular changes through a managed process that includes expert interpretation and application of subscription-based updates from ISO, NCCI, and independent state bureaus.
- **Centralized Content Repository**: Access bureau content via a centralized system that reduces configuration effort, ensures consistency, and helps you stay current — all with less IT involvement.

### Update Adoption Control

- **Adopt Bureau Circular Changes**: On your schedule, rate company, state, and effective date — giving you control over what goes live and when.

### Custom Configuration Preservation

- **Preserve Business-Specific Rating Logic and Forms**: Preserve business-specific rating logic and forms across bureau updates, ensuring configurations remain intact without the need for manual rework.

### P&C Insurance Solutions

- **Streamline Insurance Administration**: Take control of the entire insurance lifecycle with an integrated suite of solutions that unifies policy, billing, and claims on a single platform.
- **Policy Administration**: Transform policy operations Deliver products to market quickly and manage the policy lifecycle from a comprehensive system that supports straight-through processing.
- **Billing**: Elevate billing capabilities Facilitate meaningful customer interactions with billing options for automating collection, providing payment convenience, managing commissions, and delivering account visibility.
- **Claims Administration**: Optimize claims handling Reduce loss ratios, deliver exceptional service, and improve operational efficiency with workflows and automated routing that gets claims to the right adjusters at the right time.
