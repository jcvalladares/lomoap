### How to Tackle Total Cost of Risk (TCOR)

#### Summary
Total Cost of Risk (TCOR) is a crucial metric used to evaluate the effectiveness of risk management strategies across the entire organization. While it can be a daunting task to calculate and align with specific cost elements, an efficient RMIS system can significantly reduce administrative hurdles and improve the accuracy and efficiency of the calculation. This post explores the benefits of TCOR, the challenges it faces, and how a well-designed RMIS can overcome these obstacles to drive meaningful risk management improvements.

#### Key Points

1. **Definition**: Understanding what TCOR means is essential for effectively evaluating risk management processes.
   
2. **Benefits**:
   - Measuring progress towards risk management goals
   - Communicating accountability to senior management
   - Developing a more focused risk and insurance strategy
   
3. **Challenges**:
   - Lack of a unified data source
   - Time-consuming data collection and processing
   - Difficulty in accurately capturing and analyzing risk characteristics
   
4. **Solution**:
   - Utilizing a Risk Management Information System (RMIS)
   
5. **Implementation**:
   - Identifying all TCOR elements
   - Collecting accurate data from various sources
   - Choosing appropriate benchmarks
   - Ensuring alignment with strategic goals
   
6. **Key Takeaways**:
   - Aligning TCOR with risk management objectives
   - Leveraging an RMIS to reduce administrative burdens
   - Optimizing risk management processes for cost savings and better decision-making
