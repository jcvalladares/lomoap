# Cherokee Insurance Company Selects Origami Risk's Cloud-Native Core P&C Platform

## Summary

Cherokee Insurance Company, a provider of casualty insurance coverages, has selected Origami Risk's cloud-native Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform to transform its core processes, including policy administration, billing, and claims. The platform offers versatility, scalability, and security, enabling the company to enhance operations and improve decision-making. The company plans to integrate the platform seamlessly with its existing systems and deliver elevated service to policyholders, agents, and business partners. The adoption of the platform is expected to lead to increased efficiency and customer satisfaction.
