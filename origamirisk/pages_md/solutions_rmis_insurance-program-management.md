# Origami Risk's Insurance Program Management Solution

## Overview
Origami Risk's Insurance Program Management solution streamlines policy tracking, premium monitoring, and exposure management, providing a comprehensive suite of tools to enhance insurance operations and increase efficiency. It offers purpose-built tools for policy tracking, premium tracking, and exposure management, as well as advanced analytics and integrations with other systems.

## Key Features
- **Policy Tracking**: Capture and organize policy details, including terms, limits, perils, exclusions, endorsements, claims, and named insureds.
- **Premium Tracking**: Monitor premiums, fees, taxes, and mid-year changes across policies and programs.
- **Policy Erosion Analytics**: Visualize how claims impact available coverage over time.
- **Counterparty Exposure**: Evaluate carrier participation and risk transfer percentages across the portfolio.
- **Perils and Exclusions**: Document and report on named perils and exclusions for each policy.
- **Gaps and Overlaps**: Map coverage across policies to identify gaps and overlaps, project future coverage issues, and support renewal negotiations.
- **AM Best Integration**: Display AM Best ratings directly on policy records to evaluate insurer financial strength and creditworthiness.
- **Insurance Programs and Modeling**: Build and model insurance programs by grouping policies and testing coverage structures.
- **Contract Management**: Store and manage all of your organization’s insurance-related contracts with version control and access permissions to support audits and ensure consistency.
- **Certificates of Insurance (COI)**: With OCR, track vendor and contractor COIs, flag expirations and deficiencies, and streamline intake.
- **Automated Third-Party Communications**: Automate workflows and save time by using email templates to send renewal reminders, deficiency alerts, and certificate requests to brokers and vendors.
- **End-to-end Risk Management Capabilities**: Connect data for informed insights, improve incident processing, claims and policy management, renewals activities, and more.
