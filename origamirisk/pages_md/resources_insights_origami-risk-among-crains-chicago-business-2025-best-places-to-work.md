### Origami Risk Named as "Best Places to Work" in Chicago

#### Summary
Origami Risk, a leading risk, safety, and insurance software as a service (SaaS) firm, has been recognized as one of the best places to work in Chicago by Crain's Chicago Business. This prestigious accolade follows the firm's recognition by NABR and Inc. magazine. The recognition underscores Origami Risk's commitment to hiring and retaining top talent in technology, insurance, and risk management, and providing colleagues with a collaborative culture and the support they need to deliver the highest level of service to clients.

#### Key Highlights
- **Recognition**: Named as one of the "Best Places to Work" in Chicago by Crain's Chicago Business.
- **Industry Recognition**: Also recognized by the National Association of Business Resources (NABR) and Inc. magazine.
- **Honors**: Has earned more than 50 honors for workplace culture, product innovation, and client service.
- **Focus on Talent**: Emphasizes fostering a culture of collaboration and support.
- **Data-Oriented Approach**: Uses a cloud-native platform to optimize workflows for better data, insights, and collaboration.

#### Impact
The recognition highlights Origami Risk's ability to attract and retain top talent while delivering high-quality solutions to clients. It also positions the firm as a leader in the industry, setting a benchmark for excellence in workplace culture, product development, and customer service.

#### Future Focus
Origami Risk continues to focus on improving its products and solutions to meet the evolving needs of its clients. The company aims to build a strong safety culture and provide comprehensive risk management solutions that empower organizations to build for the future.
