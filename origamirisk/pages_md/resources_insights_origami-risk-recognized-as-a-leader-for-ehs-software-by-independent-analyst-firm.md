## Origami Risk Recognized as a Leader for EHS Software by Independent Analyst Firm

### Summary
Origami Risk, a leading provider of risk, safety, and insurance software as a service (SaaS), has been recognized as a leader in the Verdantix Green Quadrant: EHS Software 2025 report. The company's EHS solution advanced to the "leaders" quadrant from its prior position in the "innovators" quadrant, reflecting the solution's rapid growth in clientele and the increasing maturity and expansion of its EHS capabilities. The report praised Origami for providing a distinctive combination of solutions aimed at serving as a single source of truth for risk management, with offerings across RMIS, GRC, and EHS.

### Key Features
- **Mobile App**: A new mobile risk and safety app launched by Origami at the start of 2024 was rapidly deployed by more than 150 of its clients.
- **Advanced Capabilities**: The EHS suite is offered on a fully integrated platform for risk and safety, driving collaboration and offering more comprehensive insights into safety program impacts on enterprise and insurable risks.
- **Cloud-Based Solution**: The platform is scalable, cloud-based, and offers a range of features including safety management, PSM, chemical management, environmental and regulatory compliance, and occupational health.
- **Innovative Solutions**: The company continues to work closely with the tens of thousands of global safety professionals using the Origami platform to expand and refine its functionality, adding new capabilities to help clients collaborate effectively across their enterprises to drive speed, efficiency, transparency, continuous improvement, and best practices in EHS and risk.

### Additional Details
- **Founded in 2007**: Verdantix is an independent research and advisory firm that acts as an essential thought-leader for world-enhancing innovation.
- **Research Report**: The analysis for the report encompassed live product demonstrations, vendor responses to a 323-point questionnaire, vendor customer interviews, and data from its global survey of 301 EHS decision-makers.
