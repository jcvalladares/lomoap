# Smarter Safety Decisions: Origami's Safety Risk Assessment Solution

## Introduction

Origami Risk's Safety Risk Assessment solution is designed to empower EHS leaders to proactively identify and mitigate hazards, leading to safer workplaces and improved overall safety performance. This solution offers clear, consistent, and confident capabilities for job safety analysis (JSA), audits, and root cause analysis (RCA). By focusing on key safety workflows, the solution provides executives with executive visibility and secure buy-in, aligns safety efforts with enterprise risk strategy, and ensures proactive reduction of injuries and workplace safety.

## Key Features

### Job Safety Analysis (JSA)

- **Clarity and Consistency**: Provides a structured approach to analyzing tasks, ensuring that hazards are identified and addressed systematically.
- **Executive Visibility**: Allows EHS leaders to gain real-time visibility into ongoing safety activities and make informed decisions.
- **Secure Buy-In**: Ensures that safety measures receive the necessary support and endorsement.

### Audits

- **Hazard Identification**: Identifies potential hazards during inspections, helping to prioritize risks effectively.
- **Risk Prioritization**: Helps to determine which hazards require immediate attention.
- **Secure Buy-In**: Ensures that safety measures receive the necessary support and endorsement.

### Root Cause Analysis (RCA)

- **Contributing Risks**: Assesses the causes of incidents and identifies ways to prevent recurrence.
- **Secure Buy-In**: Ensures that safety measures receive the necessary support and endorsement.

## How It Works

1. **Job Safety Analysis (JSA)**:
   - **Task Analysis**: Break down tasks into detailed steps.
   - **Risk Evaluation**: Identify potential hazards and their severity.
   - **Recommendations**: Provide recommendations for mitigation strategies.

2. **Audits**:
   - **Inspection**: Conduct a thorough inspection of facilities and equipment.
   - **Identify Hazards**: Identify potential hazards and their severity.
   - **Prioritize Risks**: Determine which hazards require immediate attention.

3. **Root Cause Analysis (RCA)**:
   - **Incident Investigation**: Investigate the cause of incidents.
   - **Identify Contributing Risks**: Determine the factors that contributed to the incident.
   - **Mitigation Strategies**: Develop strategies to prevent recurrence.
