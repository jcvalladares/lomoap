# Origami Risk 2025 RMIS Report: Leader in Risk Management and Integrated Risk Management

## Summary
Origami Risk, a leading risk and insurance software provider, has been recognized as a "Leader" in the 2025 edition of Redhand Advisor's RMIS Report. This year, Origami Risk achieved the highest overall "System Satisfaction" score and the top Net Promoter Score (NPS). The company's system capabilities rank "Excellent" to "Above Average" across all 11 categories, with "Excellent" ratings in eight categories. According to the report, organizations increasingly need systems that integrate seamlessly with business applications for centralized data management and provide a unified view of risk across claims, safety and compliance functions. Origami Risk delivers inherently integrated data, reporting, and workflows accessible through a single user interface, making it the only vendor offering a complete IRM solution on one platform.
