# Origami Risk Recognized in Gartner's Magic Quadrant for Governance, Risk and Compliance Tools, Assurance Leaders

## Summary
Origami Risk, a cloud-native platform for risk management, has been recognized in the 2025 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Governance, Risk and Compliance Tools, Assurance Leaders. The company offers AI-driven capabilities to enhance risk identification, analysis, and control, enabling assurance leaders to reduce manual effort and improve framework alignment. The platform supports multiple industries and provides a comprehensive set of end-to-end solutions for policy, rating, billing, loss control, claims administration, reporting and analytics.

## Key Features
- **AI Risk and Control Explorer**: Leverages artificial intelligence to accelerate risk identification and control mapping.
- **Full Suite of Solutions**: Includes policy, rating, billing, loss control, claims administration, reporting and analytics, as well as ISO and NCCI bureau content management and digital engagement experience.
- **Continuous Innovation**: Grounded in continuous innovation and a foundational focus on client success.
- **Trust and Resilience**: Trusted by leading organizations to enable greater resilience as they build for the future.

## Benefits
- **Improved Data and Insights**: Highly configurable solutions integrate on a single platform, providing optimized workflows for better data, better insights, and better collaboration.
- **Reduced Manual Effort**: Artificial intelligence capabilities reduce manual effort, improving framework alignment and audit readiness.
- **Comprehensive Solutions**: Supports multiple industries and provides a comprehensive set of end-to-end solutions for risk, safety, and insurance.
