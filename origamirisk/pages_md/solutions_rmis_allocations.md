## Origami Risk Allocations

### Overview
Origami Risk provides comprehensive risk management solutions for various industries, including construction, government, healthcare, manufacturing, and transportation. Their platform offers advanced tools for managing risk costs, driving accountability, and aligning financial impact with risk ownership.

### Key Features
1. **Centralized Risk Management**: Centralizes insurable risk data, facilitating efficient management and reporting.
2. **TCOR Analytics**: Enables seamless integration with Origami Risk’s platform, providing dynamic AI-powered calculations and analytics.
3. **End-to-end Risk Management Capabilities**: Integrates data for informed insights, accelerating claims resolution, improving incident and event management, and optimizing insurance program management.
4. **Allocation Models**: Supports customizable allocation methods and forecasting, ensuring alignment with organizational structures and risk goals.
5. **Statistics**: Provides real-time statistics and analytics, helping in measuring and analyzing risk costs.
6. **Marketing**: Offers targeted advertising and tracking capabilities for enhanced marketing efforts.
