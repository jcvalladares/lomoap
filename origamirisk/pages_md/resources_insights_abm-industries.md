### ABM Industries: Transforming Risk & Safety with Origami Risk

#### Overview
ABM Industries embarked on a comprehensive transformation initiative, Vision 2020, to unify operations and drive consistent excellence across its organization. This case study explores how the Risk & Safety department implemented strategic initiatives to enhance safety measures and reduce claims frequency and severity. By leveraging Origami Risk's RMIS, EHS, and GRC suites, ABM achieved significant improvements in risk management and safety culture.

#### Key Achievements
- **Improved Safety Measures**: Implemented programs like "Moment for Safety" and claims closure efforts.
- **Reduced Claim Frequency and Severity**: Achieved a 35% decrease in casualty claim frequency rate and an 18.5% reduction in total cost of risk over a five-year period.
- **Enhanced Data Consolidation**: Leveraged Origami Risk's RMIS to consolidate data and streamline processes.

#### Technology Integration
- **Origami Risk RMIS**: Used for managing insurable risk data.
- **Origami Risk EHS**: Supported operational risk management.
- **Origami Risk GRC**: Managed governance, risk, and compliance.

#### Benefits
- **Streamlined Operations**: Reduced manual processes and improved efficiency.
- **Data-Driven Decision Making**: Enhanced insights and better decision-making based on data-driven strategies.
- **Customer Satisfaction**: Improved customer satisfaction through reduced claims and enhanced safety measures.

#### Conclusion
The successful implementation of Origami Risk's platforms has significantly transformed ABM Industries' risk and safety departments. The company now operates with a unified approach to risk management, leading to improved outcomes and a stronger safety culture. ABM Industries remains committed to continuous improvement and innovation, ensuring that it continues to meet the evolving needs of its customers and stakeholders.
