# Origami Risk: A Comprehensive Platform for Risk, Safety, and Compliance

## Overview
Origami Risk is a cutting-edge platform designed to enhance operations, streamline processes, and improve risk management for various industries. Built on top of the latest technology, it offers seamless integration with your data sources, expert system configuration and guidance, and intuitive apps for on-the-go reporting. Our platform provides customizable solutions for risk, safety, and insurance, empowering organizations to adapt, respond, and lead through change.

### Key Features
- **Intelligent Innovation**: Our platform drives innovation and automates repetitive tasks, reducing manual work and increasing efficiency.
- **Unified Platform**: Centralizes risk, safety, and compliance data across multiple systems, eliminating silos and improving visibility.
- **Automated Workflows**: Automates reporting, notifications, and compliance tracking to ensure timely action.
- **Real-Time Insights**: Monitors leading indicators and responds quickly with dashboards and analytics.
- **Role-Based Access**: Securely manages data access based on roles and permissions.
- **Flexible Data Intake**: Supports flexible, structured intake for better data quality and readiness.
- **Scale with Confidence**: Adapts quickly with configurable tools and modular design.
- **Enterprise-grade Security**: Ensures robust security with role-based access, audit trails, and compliance with SOC 2, ISO 27001, and GDPR.
- **Integration with Multiple Systems**: Seamless integration with P&C Insurance, RMIS, EHS, GRC, Healthcare, Construction, Government, and Manufacturing.
- **Case Studies**: Real-world success stories from Origami Risk clients demonstrating their ability to drive safety and savings.

## Solutions
- **Risk Management Information Systems (RMIS)**: Centralize and manage insurable risk data.
- **TCOR Analytics**: Claims management, incidents & events, program management, allocations, and exposure management.
- **Environment, Health & Safety (EHS)**: Power a strong safety culture with consistent standards, training, and reporting.
- **Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)**: Ensure compliance, maintain policies, and track risks.
- **Business Continuity Management**: Enterprise risk management, internal audit, internal controls, policy & procedure management, and third-party risk management.
- **Healthcare Solutions**: Integrated solutions for healthcare providers.
