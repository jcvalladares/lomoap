## McCarthy Building Companies, Inc.: A Comprehensive Risk Management Solution

### Introduction
McCarthy Building Companies, Inc., a leading builder with operations across diverse construction sectors, sought a unified, automated system to streamline their manual risk management processes. This case study explores how they leveraged Origami Risk's highly configurable platform to achieve improved risk visibility, enhanced compliance oversight, and streamlined risk management.

### Case Study: McCarthy Building Companies' Risk Management Transformation
#### Problem Statement
McCarthy had a manual risk management system that was inefficient, prone to errors, and lacked the ability to integrate with other systems. They needed a solution that could automate risk assessments, capture evidence, and generate actionable insights.

#### Solution Implementation
McCarthy implemented Origami Risk's platform, which featured automated questionnaires, OCR-enabled certificate processing, and project risk assessment capabilities. These tools allowed them to quickly identify and score project-specific risks while maintaining consistency with compliance standards.

#### Key Features and Benefits
- **Automated Questionnaires**: McCarthy used Origami Risk's AI-powered questionnaires to gather detailed risk information from employees.
- **OCR-Enabled Certificate Processing**: The platform supported OCR technology for capturing certificates and documents automatically.
- **Project Risk Assessment**: Users could easily assess risks associated with different projects using the platform's intuitive interface.
- **Improved Visibility**: Automated risk scoring and reporting helped McCarthy gain real-time insights into their risk landscape.
- **Enhanced Compliance Oversight**: The platform ensured compliance with regulatory requirements by providing a centralized view of risk status.
- **Streamlined Risk Management**: The implementation reduced manual effort and increased efficiency in managing risks.

### Conclusion
By leveraging Origami Risk's platform, McCarthy Building Companies achieved significant improvements in their risk management processes. The platform's automation capabilities enabled them to streamline risk assessments, improve compliance oversight, and enhance overall risk management effectiveness. This case study demonstrates how a well-designed risk management solution can significantly reduce costs and increase productivity for organizations like McCarthy Building Companies.
