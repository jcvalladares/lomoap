# Origami Risk: A Comprehensive Platform for Internal Controls Management

## Overview
Origami Risk is an innovative platform that enhances operations by providing a seamless integration with various data sources, expert system configuration and guidance, and customizable solutions for risk, safety, and insurance. The platform offers a range of tools, including Origami Mobile for on-the-go reporting, configurable dashboards, and purpose-built solutions for improving outcomes. Additionally, it supports centralized control management, AI-driven risk analysis, and other advanced features to help organizations build more resilient businesses.
