## Healthcare Provider Performance Solution

### Overview
Origami Risk provides a comprehensive solution designed to elevate provider performance, enhance patient safety, and proactively monitor, evaluate, and improve provider quality. Our platform integrates OPPE, FPPE, and Peer Review functionality on a single platform with tools for assigning coordinators, routing reviews to specialty teams, launching evaluations from safety events, and managing structured improvement plans. This solution helps organizations meet compliance requirements, reduces risk, and drives better outcomes for their patients.

### Key Features
1. **Integrated Toolkit**: Offers a comprehensive set of tools for provider performance management.
2. **Focused Professional Practice Evaluation (FPPE)**: Provides time-bound evaluations for new providers or those under review.
3. **Ongoing Professional Practice Evaluation (OPPE)**: Continuously monitors provider performance using real-time data and configurable dashboards.
4. **Peer Review**: Conducts structured, confidential peer reviews with customizable templates and workflows.
5. **Other Healthcare Solutions**: Eliminates silos with a unified healthcare system, strengthens risk management, patient safety, and quality programs with configurable solutions tailored to individual needs.
6. **Patient Safety & Quality**: Includes features for claims management and administration, employee health and safety, patient safety and quality achievements, and claim management and administration.
