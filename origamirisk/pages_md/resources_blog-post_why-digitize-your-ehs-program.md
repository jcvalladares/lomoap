### Webinar Recap: The True Impact of Digital in EHS

#### Summary
In this webinar, we explored the role of digital transformation in enhancing operational efficiency and safety in the EHS sector. Key points discussed included:

- **Digital Transformation**: Definition and benefits of digital transformation, including better management of data, ensuring regulatory compliance, reducing paper usage, and improving risk understanding.
  
- **Types of Digital Transformation**:
  - **Process Transformation**: Automating back-office processes.
  - **Business Model Transformation**: Redefining the car insurance business model.
  - **Domain Transformation**: Unlocking new revenue opportunities.
  
- **Examples of EHS Technologies**:
  - Enterprise-wide views and management system frameworks.
  - Communication tools.
  - Virtual assessment tools.
  - Advanced reporting and predictive/prescriptive analytics.
  - Intuitive and easy-to-use mobile apps.
  - Industrial wearables and connected worker solutions.

- **Impact of Digital Transformation**:
  - Improved performance through streamlined data management and standardized processes.
  - Proactive risk control, workflow automation, and standardization.
  - Enhanced data capture and auditability.
  - Time savings, improved accuracy, and data quality.
  - Increased transparency, visibility, and accountability.
  - Integration of systems and real-time tracking.
  - Increased brand reputation and proactive safety culture.
  
- **Origami's EHS Suite**:
  - Pre-configured audit, inspection, and behavior-based safety observation checklists based on industry best practices and OSHA standards.
  - Incident management and investigations, including root cause analysis.
  - Dashboards and reporting.
  - Job Safety Analysis (JSA).
  - Safety meetings.

- **Key Takeaways**:
  - EHS investments are growing at 10% annually.
  - Notable investments include incident management, safety hazards & risk mitigation, occupational health, mental health, and wellbeing monitoring.
  - Mobile adoption is increasing.
  - Companies can leverage digital EHS technology to improve safety culture and drive business outcomes.

This webinar provided valuable insights into the strategic importance of digital transformation in the EHS sector and how Origami's suite can support organizations in achieving these goals.
