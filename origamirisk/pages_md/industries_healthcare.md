# Healthcare Risk Management Software

## Overview

Origami Risk offers a range of healthcare risk management software solutions designed to streamline operations, improve patient safety, and ensure compliance. Our platform provides customizable solutions for risk, safety, and insurance, empowering organizations to protect their patients, staff, and assets.

### Key Features

- **Patient Safety and Quality**: Our solutions include robust claims management and administration, provider performance, employee health and safety, patient safety and quality.
- **Claims Management and Administration**: Streamlines healthcare claims from FNOL to closure, consolidates data, and optimizes workflow.
- **Provider Performance**: Supports excellence in clinical practice, measures performance, and tracks progress against industry standards.
- **Environment, Health and Safety (EHS)**: Manages hazards, tracks incidents, and ensures compliance with environmental and occupational health standards.
- **Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC)**: Power enterprise risk management, compliance, and vendor risk management programs.
- **Case Studies**: Showcase real-world success stories from Origami Risk clients.
- **Insights**: Provide industry reports, articles, how-to guides, and more.
- **Events**: Connect, learn, and network in person or online.
- **About**: Learn about Origami Risk's mission and support for the risk and insurance ecosystem.
