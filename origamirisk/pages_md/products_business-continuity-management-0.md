# GRC | Business Continuity Management (BCM)

## Overview

Origami Risk is a powerful platform designed to enhance operations by providing seamless integration with data sources, expert system configuration, and purpose-built solutions for risk, safety, and insurance. Our platform offers a range of offerings including business continuity planning, disaster response, and continuous improvement initiatives. With its focus on speed, scalability, and security, Origami Risk empowers organizations to build more resilient businesses.

## Key Features

### Business Continuity Planning
- **Coordinate continuity efforts**: Utilizes connected tools to manage continuity plans across different departments.
- **Crisis Response**: Supports proactive planning and response through task tracking, communication tools, and scenario-based planning.
- **Disaster Response**: Launches incident management workflows with automated notifications and step-by-step task execution.
- **Issue Management**: Identifies and resolves gaps in the continuity program.

### Other GRC SOLUTIONS
- **Connect your risk programs**: Elevates insight of risk impacts to an enterprise-level understanding with an integrated solution tailored to meet your organization's requirements and workflows.
- **Third-Party Risk Management**: Mitigates vendor and contractor liability using tools for identifying, assessing, and mitigating risks across your entire third-party network.
- **Policy & Procedure Management**: Drive effective policy and procedure management.
- **Internal Controls Management**: Understand internal risk control effectiveness and test and manage controls across departments.
- **Compliance Management**: Measure and report on organizational compliance.
