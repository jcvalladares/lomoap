# Enterprise Risk Management Software

## Overview

Platform One offers an innovative platform designed for speed, scalability, and security. It includes AI tools for boosting speed, insight, and impact, seamless integration with data sources, expert system configuration and guidance, and an intuitive mobile app for on-the-go reporting. Our platform supports various risk management solutions including P&C Insurance Cloud-native tools, purpose-built solutions for risk, safety, and insurance, and comprehensive solutions for healthcare providers. It also provides tools for aligning risk planning, response, and performance, managing corporate objectives, categorizing risks, identifying threats, prioritizing risks, controlling risks, resolving issues, and implementing governance, risk, and compliance strategies. 

## Features

### Risk Management Information Systems (RMIS)

Centralize and manage insurable risk data with our RMIS solutions, which include corporate objectives, risk hierarchy and register, risk identification, risk assessment, control management, issue management, AI risk and control explorer, and other tools.

### Environment, Health & Safety (EHS)

Power a strong safety culture with our EHS solutions, which include operational risk management, governance, risk & compliance, business continuity management, third-party risk management, policy & procedure management, internal controls management, compliance management, and more.

### Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)

Identify, assess, and mitigate enterprise risk with our GRC solutions, which include business continuity management, third-party risk management, policy & procedure management, internal controls management, compliance management, and more.

### Healthcare Solutions

Integrate solutions for healthcare providers, which include integrated solutions for healthcare providers, integrated risk management (IRM), unified risk, safety, and compliance on one platform, and more.

### Industries

Empower your organization with solutions that streamline operations and turn data into actionable insights for various industries, including P&C Insurance, Construction, Government, Healthcare, Manufacturing, and Transportation.
