### 5 ERM Lessons You Don't Have to Learn the Hard Way

#### Introduction
This webinar offers five critical lessons that risk leaders often learn the hard way when building an effective Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) program. By understanding these lessons, organizations can avoid common pitfalls and achieve greater success.

#### Key Takeaways
1. **Identifying Champions**: Emphasize the importance of empowering individuals who are committed to driving a risk-aware culture within the organization.
   
2. **Filtering Noise**: Focus on filtering out irrelevant and noisy data to focus on meaningful risk registers.

3. **Moving from Vague Risks to Measurable Insights**: Transform vague, undocumented risks into thoughtful, measurable insights using data-driven decision-making techniques.

4. **Shift from Documentation to Mitigation**: Prioritize moving from documenting risks to implementing mitigation strategies to ensure effective risk management.

5. **Positioning Your ERM Program for Resilience**: Ensure that your ERM program is designed to build resilience and adapt to changing environments.
