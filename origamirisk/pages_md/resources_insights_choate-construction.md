### Title: Origami Risk: A Comprehensive Risk Management Solution for Construction

#### Summary:
Origami Risk is a cloud-based platform designed to enhance operations by providing a scalable, secure, and efficient solution for managing risk, safety, and insurance. It offers a range of features tailored to different industries, including construction, healthcare, and manufacturing, helping organizations streamline their risk management processes and achieve greater success.

#### Key Features:
- **Centralized Risk Management**: Ensures accurate and accountability for subcontractor compliance, insurance tracking, and contract approvals.
- **Real-Time Visibility**: Offers custom dashboards and automated reports for real-time visibility across offices.
- **Automated Processes**: Reduces manual or spreadsheet-based processes and automates compliance-related tasks.
- **Transparency**: Improves visibility and accountability through transparent compliance reviews.
- **Collaboration**: Facilitates collaboration between compliance and operations teams.
- **Continuous Improvement**: Supports ongoing growth and innovation by integrating with other platforms and updating dashboards.

#### Success Stories:
- **Choate Construction**: Centralizes risk management operations, improving compliance, reducing project delays, and enhancing profitability.
- **Global Materials Corporation**: Uses Origami Risk to manage subcontractor limits and aggregate exposure, strengthening risk management and improving rates.

#### Integration Capabilities:
- **TradeTap**: Captures compliance scoring and ensures timely renewals.
- **Vista**: Integrates with the platform to review subcontractor details, insurance certificates, and incident reports.
- **BuildingConnected**: Configures advanced dashboards for reporting and oversight.

#### Future Plans:
- **Advanced Dashboards**: Configuring advanced dashboards to support reporting and oversight.
- **Vendor Management**: Introducing vendor management capabilities.
- **Integrations with Other Platforms**: Expanding integrations with other tools and systems.

#### Conclusion:
Origami Risk is a powerful tool for construction companies looking to streamline risk management, safety, and insurance processes. Its comprehensive suite of features and continuous improvement capabilities make it an essential component of any modern construction operation.
