# GRC | Policy & Procedure Management

## Overview
Origami Risk provides a comprehensive suite of tools to streamline corporate policy management, ensuring consistency, control, and compliance across your organization. With its platform, you can centralize policy drafting, review, and distribution on one platform, improving efficiency and reducing manual effort. Our policy management solution offers robust features for issues management, policy repository, and attestation collection, helping you maintain accurate and up-to-date policies.

## Key Features
### Tools for Managing Policies
- **Policy Management Draft, Edit, and Approve**: Draft, edit, and approve policy documents using configurable templates and workflows.
- **Maintain Version Control and Audit Trails**: Ensure transparency and traceability through version control and audit trails.
- **Issues Management**: Identify gaps in your policy program and assign action plans.
- **Policy Repository**: Store and organize policies in a centralized, searchable database.
- **Role-Based Access**: Ensure employees and contractors see only the latest approved versions.
- **Attestation Collection and Tracking**: Automate the collection and tracking of employee attestations with reminders and real-time reporting.
- **Link Attestations to Policies**: Create a complete audit-ready record.

### Other GRC SOLUTIONS
- **Connect Your Risk Programs with Purpose-Built GRC Capabilities**: Elevate insight of risk impacts to an enterprise-level understanding with an integrated solution tailored to meet your organization's requirements and workflows.
- **Business Continuity Management**: Increase operational resilience by building and managing continuity plans that mitigate operational threats and minimize financial impacts by ensuring swift recovery following disruptions.
- **Third-Party Risk Management**: Mitigate vendor and contractor liability using tools for identifying, assessing, and mitigating risks across your entire third-party network.

## Additional Benefits
- **Automated Workflows and Attestation Tracking**: Reduce manual effort, improve visibility, and help compliance leaders demonstrate readiness during audits.
- **Centralized Policy Data Management**: Centralize and manage insurable risk data, streamlining operations and enhancing decision-making.
- **Integration with Data Sources**: Seamless integration with your data sources, allowing for efficient data sharing and analysis.
- **Intuitive App for On-the-Go Reporting**: An intuitive app for on-the-go reporting, providing quick access to critical data and insights.
