### Title: Public Employer Risk Management Association, Inc. (PERMA) Case Study: Utilizing Origami Risk's Platform to Streamline Claims Administration

#### Summary:
This case study explores how PERMA, a public employer risk management association, successfully implemented Origami Risk's platform to streamline its claims administration operations. The company faced the challenge of managing claims in-house while enhancing efficiency and member value. By leveraging Origami Risk's configurable platform, PERMA achieved significant time savings, improved data accuracy, and increased member satisfaction, all while meeting tight implementation timelines.

#### Key Takeaways:
- **Streamlined Claims Administration:** PERMA was able to significantly reduce claim processing times by automating repetitive tasks.
- **Enhanced Communication:** The platform facilitated seamless communication between employees, managers, and other stakeholders.
- **Improved Data Accuracy:** The platform helped ensure accurate data entry and tracking, leading to reduced errors and fraud.
- **Increased Member Satisfaction:** Improved operational efficiency led to higher member satisfaction levels.
- **Tailored Solutions:** The platform provided customizable solutions tailored to each organization's needs, enhancing overall risk management effectiveness.

#### Impact on Operations:
The implementation of Origami Risk's platform resulted in a 30% reduction in claim processing time, which saved the company $100,000 annually. Additionally, the platform improved data accuracy, reducing the number of errors and fraud cases by 70%. These improvements translated into increased member satisfaction, resulting in higher retention rates and lower employee turnover.

#### Conclusion:
PERMA's successful implementation of Origami Risk's platform demonstrated the power of technology in streamlining complex operations and enhancing risk management outcomes. The platform's flexibility and customization capabilities made it an ideal solution for organizations looking to improve their claims administration processes while maintaining high standards of quality and efficiency.
