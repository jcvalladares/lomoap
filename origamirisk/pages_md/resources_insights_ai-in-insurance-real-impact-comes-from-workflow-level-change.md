# AI in Insurance: Real Impact Comes from Workflow-Level Change

## Overview
Insurance AI is transforming the way insurers operate by leveraging advanced technology to enhance speed, accuracy, and customer experience. By focusing on workflow-level change, insurers avoid the pitfalls of stalled pilots and endless experimentation. This approach starts small, builds confidence, and scales what works. The result is a path to measurable improvements today, with a foundation for broader adoption tomorrow.

## Key Workflows
1. **First Notice of Loss (FNOL)**: AI accelerates the process by extracting and classifying unstructured data, reducing manual triage and speeding assignment.
2. **Reserve Validation and Outcome Prediction**: Predicts likely settlement outcomes and highlights discrepancies, improving trust in the model before integrating reserve alerts into live workflows.
3. **Spotting High-Risk Claims Early**: Uses dynamic risk scores to prioritize resource allocation, ensuring proactive intervention.
4. **Detecting Fraud**: Analyzes patterns in claims history and outcomes to surface fraudulent activity, preventing runaway costs.
5. **Streamlining Claims Communication**: Automates drafting and approval of status letters, improving efficiency and consistency.
