# Smarter Safety Strategies

## Introduction

In today's fast-paced world, maintaining a safe work environment is essential for ensuring the health and well-being of employees. The Origami Risk platform offers a suite of tools and solutions designed to enhance safety, efficiency, and effectiveness in various industries. This session explores advanced risk assessment techniques that help prioritize safety efforts, allocate resources effectively, and address high-impact hazards to foster a safer workplace.

### Key Takeaways

1. **Prioritization of Safety Efforts**: Conducting thorough risk assessments helps in identifying and addressing high-risk hazards early.
2. **Engaging Executive Leadership**: Effective communication through persuasive communication enables alignment and support for safety initiatives.
3. **Enhancing Collaboration**: Integrating EHS teams with other departments fosters better reporting, accountability, and shared safety outcomes.
4. **Technology for Enhanced Visibility**: Modern tools and techniques increase organizational visibility, streamline communication, and support the consistent implementation and monitoring of safety measures.
5. **Marketing for Better Incident and Claims Management**: Leveraging data, technology, and strategic collaboration drives better incident and claims management.
