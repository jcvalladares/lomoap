# American Guaranty Fund Group Selects Origami Risk's Cloud-Native Platform to Transform Claims at Florida's Guaranty Associations

## Summary
American Guaranty Fund Group has selected Origami Risk's cloud-native core P&C platform to enhance claims administration at Florida's Guaranty Associations. The platform aims to modernize the claims processing capabilities of FIGA and FWCIGA, improving speed, accuracy, and efficiency in claim adjudication and cost savings. The company leverages automation and streamlined workflows to achieve these objectives. This upgrade is part of a broader effort to standardize and streamline the claim processes at the state associations, driving efficiencies, reducing costs, and improving the overall customer experience.

## Impact
This upgrade is expected to significantly improve the operational efficiency and customer satisfaction of Florida's Guaranty Associations. By leveraging advanced technology, the company aims to enhance their ability to protect policyholders and drive sustainable growth in the insurance market.
