# Origami Risk: Modernizing Incident Reporting and Strengthening Safety Culture

## Overview
Moran, a leading marine towing and transportation services provider, embarked on a journey to enhance its risk management system. After evaluating several technology solutions, Origami Risk emerged as the chosen partner due to its sales team's responsiveness and reputation for excellent service. By streamlining incident reporting and improving employee engagement, Moran achieved significant improvements in near-miss reporting and data quality.

## Key Features and Benefits
- **Cloud-Native RMIS Solution**: Centralizes and manages insurable risk data, enabling real-time visibility and standardization.
- **Mobile Reporting and Anonymous Collection Links**: Allows crew members to report near misses and hazards directly from their personal devices, enhancing data quality and turnaround time.
- **Empowering Employees**: Enables port managers to enter incident data more efficiently, fostering a culture of psychological safety.
- **Data-Driven Safety Programs**: Utilizes analytics to identify trends and proactively address emerging risks, leveraging Origami Risk dashboards for monitoring performance.
- **Claims and Certificates of Insurance**: Centralizes claims data and supports self-administration, improving efficiency and visibility.

## Impact
Over the past decade, Moran has experienced a 180% increase in near miss reporting, demonstrating employee empowerment and improved operational safety.

## Future Plans
The organization continues to expand and refine its use of Origami Risk, focusing on standardizing data collection across its business units and building more internal expertise. Additionally, the team is exploring integrations with third-party administrations (TPAs) and insurers to further streamline claims processes and enhance visibility.

## Conclusion
Origami Risk's modernized incident reporting and strengthened safety culture have had a significant positive impact on Moran's operations and employee satisfaction. Its commitment to continuous improvement and employee engagement ensures that the organization remains a leader in the risk and insurance ecosystem.
