# Risk Management Information Systems (RMIS)

## Overview

Origami Risk's Risk Management Information Systems (RMIS) platform offers a comprehensive suite of tools to enhance operations, improve outcomes, and protect against risks. Built for speed, scalability, and security, Origami Risk's RMIS centralizes insurable risk data, automates workflows, and provides actionable insights to drive effective loss prevention, control claims costs, and improve renewals. With award-winning client satisfaction scores of 6 years running, Origami Risk's RMIS has become the go-to solution for organizations looking to streamline their risk management processes.

## Features

- **Centralized Visibility**: Unifies risk, safety, and insurance data within a centralized risk management system.
- **Automated Efficiency**: Improves operations with adaptable workflows that save time and ensure nothing falls through the cracks.
- **Actionable Intelligence**: Transforms data into insights using AI-driven analysis within the RMIS to reveal trends and identify opportunities.
- **End-to-end Risk Management Solutions**: Connects risk and insurance data within a centralized risk management system to gain informed insights and streamline incident processing, claims administration, policy management, renewals, and more — delivering comprehensive, end-to-end control while reducing reliance on legacy applications and multiple spreadsheets.
- **TCOR Analytics**: Measures risk investment impact with dynamic AI-powered calculations, limitless data ingestion options, flexible visualizations, actionable projection capabilities, and seamless integration with Allocations.
- **Claims Management**: Accelerates claims resolution by consolidating your claims data across all lines of coverage to streamline workflows, simplify financial reporting, identify trends, reduce litigation costs, and improve claims outcomes.
- **Incident and Event Management**: Improves incident collection and prevention by capturing accurate incident data using flexible intake tools, automate workflows and TPA feeds, and analyze root causes to understand and prevent future occurrences.
- **Insurance Program Management**: Unifies your policy information by connecting it to claims data, enabling analysis of coverage levels, retention scenarios, and counterparty exposure while simplifying renewals and broker communication.
- **Allocations**: Allocate costs with precision by allocating risk management costs across the organization based on configurable formulaic models, flexible cost element selection, and seamless integration with TCOR Analytics.
- **Exposure Management**: Track exposures efficiently by managing exposure information across location hierarchies that mirror your organizational structure, automate values collection to reduce administrative workload, and forecast exposure changes to support renewal planning.
