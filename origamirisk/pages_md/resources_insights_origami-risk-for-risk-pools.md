# Origami Risk for Risk Pools

## Overview
Platform One offers an innovative platform for risk pools, enhancing operations with speed, scalability, and security. It includes AI tools, seamless data integration, expert system configuration, mobile app, customizable dashboards, purpose-built solutions, and a wide range of industries.

## Key Features
- **AI Tools**: Boost speed, insight, and impact.
- **API Access**: Seamless integration with data sources.
- **Implementation and Support**: Expert system configuration and guidance.
- **Reporting and Analytics**: Configurable dashboards and personalized reporting.
- **Purpose-built Solutions**: Improve outcomes with configurable, integrated solutions for risk, safety, and insurance.
- **Cloud-native Tools**: Policy, billing, and claims for P&C insurance.
- **Environment, Health & Safety (EHS)**: Power a strong safety culture.
- **Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)**: Identify, assess, and mitigate enterprise risk.
- **Business Continuity Management**: Enterprise Risk Management, internal audit, internal controls, policy & procedure management, third-party risk management.
- **Healthcare Solutions**: Integrated solutions for healthcare providers.
- **Integrated Risk Management (IRM)**: Unify risk, safety, and compliance on one platform.
- **Industries**: Empower organizations with solutions for various industries.
