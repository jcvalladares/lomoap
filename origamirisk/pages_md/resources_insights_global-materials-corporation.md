### Global Materials Corporation: Risk Management Solution

#### Summary
Global Materials Corporation, a global materials company operating in more than 25 countries and running over 50 production sites, faced numerous challenges in managing their risk and insurance programs due to inefficient data collection and reporting practices. In response, they partnered with Origami Risk, an innovative platform that provided a comprehensive risk and insurance solution. By implementing Origami Risk's GRC and RMIS solutions, the company experienced a significant increase in process efficiency and a marked improvement in the visibility and management of their risk data. The solution enabled the company to accurately and swiftly allocate premium, providing clear insights into the risks and events impacting the business across the globe. The platform's centralized data management and customizable reporting capabilities significantly streamlined the risk management process and improved overall operational effectiveness.
