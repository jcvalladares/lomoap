# Origami Risk: Modernizing Incident Reporting and Strengthening Safety Culture

## Overview
Moran is a leading marine towing and transportation services provider with a history spanning over 165 years. They have developed a legacy risk management system that lacked transparency and difficulty accessing critical data. In 2015, Moran embarked on a search for a replacement solution that would improve incident reporting, employee engagement, and safety culture.

## Solution Overview
Origami Risk's cloud-native RMIS solution, powered by AI, mobile reporting, and anonymous collection links, enables Moran to streamline incident reporting and improve data quality. The solution provides configurable dashboards and reporting tools, integrates with HR, ERP, AP, and fleet systems, and includes targeted safety programs based on near-miss data.

## Key Features
1. **Mobile Reporting**: Employees can now report near misses and safety concerns directly from their personal devices using the Origami Mobile app.
2. **Dashboards**: Customizable dashboards display leading indicators and lagging indicators, helping Moran stay focused on measurable outcomes.
3. **Integration**: The system integrates with various departments within Moran, streamlining data collection and analysis.
4. **Reporting and Analytics**: Comprehensive reporting and analytics provide real-time insights into risk and safety performance.
5. **Safety Programs**: Targeted safety programs are designed to proactively address emerging risks across the fleet.

## Impact
Over the past decade, Moran has experienced a 180% increase in near miss reporting, indicating increased employee engagement and psychological safety. The company has also streamlined claims processing and enhanced visibility and standardization across business units.

## Future Plans
Moran continues to expand and refine its use of Origami Risk. They plan to standardize data collection across their environmental and industrial and marine construction business units, build more internal expertise, and explore integrations with third-party administrations and insurers to further streamline claims processes and enhance visibility.

## Conclusion
Origami Risk's modernized incident reporting and strengthened safety culture represent a significant step forward in Moran's risk management efforts. By leveraging AI, mobile reporting, and anonymous collection links, they have created a robust system that improves incident reporting, employee engagement, and safety culture.
