# Harford Mutual Insurance Group Claims Administration

## Overview

Harford Mutual Insurance Group (HMIG) has successfully transformed its multi-line claims administration system using Origami Risk's multi-tenant claims solution. The project was a significant undertaking that involved multiple departments and stakeholders, with a focus on delivering exceptional service and value to policyholders, agents, and employees.

### Key Achievements

- **Cloud Technology**: HMIG leveraged cloud technology driven by configuration over coding to bring claims under a single software.
- **Integration**: The multi-tenant claims solution integrated seamlessly with the existing policy administration system, allowing for efficient workflow management.
- **Performance**: The project was completed on time and within budget, demonstrating the effectiveness of the solution.

### Impact

The success of the project has led to increased efficiency and improved customer satisfaction, as well as a competitive edge in the market. HMIG has expanded its offerings into new areas, including workers' compensation insurance, and continues to invest in technology to stay ahead of the curve.

## Team Collaboration

The project was successful due to the collaboration between HMIG's leadership team, systems integrator Ernst & Young, and Origami Risk. The team worked closely together to define project goals, develop a roadmap, and address any challenges that arose during the implementation process.

### Lessons Learned

The experience taught HMIG the importance of stakeholder engagement and continuous improvement. The team learned to communicate effectively with all stakeholders, ensuring that everyone understood the project's objectives and the benefits of the solution.

## Future Directions

With the continued growth of the business, HMIG plans to expand its reach into new industries, including manufacturing and transportation. The company remains committed to delivering high-quality claims administration services to its customers, and is excited about the opportunities that lie ahead.

## Conclusion

The successful transformation of HMIG's claims administration system using Origami Risk's multi-tenant claims solution represents a significant step forward in the company's journey towards becoming a leading provider of comprehensive insurance services. By leveraging cloud technology and integrating with existing systems, HMIG has been able to improve efficiency, reduce costs, and enhance customer satisfaction. As the business grows, HMIG looks forward to continuing its commitment to delivering innovative solutions that meet the needs of its customers.
