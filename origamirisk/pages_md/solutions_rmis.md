### Title: Comprehensive Risk Management Solution for Insurers

#### Summary:
Origami Risk offers a robust Risk Management Information System (RMIS) designed to optimize risk management across various industries, including property and casualty (P&C) insurance. The RMIS centralizes risk data, automates workflows, and provides actionable insights to enhance operational efficiency, reduce costs, and improve outcomes. Its features include TCOR Analytics for risk investment impact, claims acceleration, incident and event management, insurance program management, allocations, and exposure tracking. With a top client satisfaction score of 6 years running, Origami Risk is recognized for its effectiveness in managing insurable risks.

#### Key Features:
- **Centralized Visibility**: Unified risk, safety, and insurance data across a centralized risk management system.
- **Automated Efficiency**: Adaptable workflows that save time and ensure no errors fall through the cracks.
- **Actionable Intelligence**: AI-driven analysis within the RMIS to reveal trends and identify opportunities.
- **End-to-end Risk Management Solutions**: Connects risk and insurance data within a single system to deliver comprehensive control.
- **TCOR Analytics**: Dynamic AI-powered calculations, limitless data ingestion options, flexible visualizations, actionable projection capabilities, and seamless integration with Allocations.
- **Claims Management**: Consolidates claims data across all lines of coverage to streamline workflows and simplify financial reporting.
- **Incident and Event Management**: Accurate incident data capture, automation workflows, and TPA feeds.
- **Insurance Program Management**: Unifies policy information and connects it to claims data.
- **Allocations**: Precision allocation of risk management costs based on configurable models.
- **Exposure Management**: Efficient tracking of exposures across location hierarchies, automated values collection, and forecasting exposure changes.
