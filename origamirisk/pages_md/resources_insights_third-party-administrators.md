## Third-Party Administrators (TPAs)

### Overview

Origami Risk is an innovative platform designed for third-party administrators (TPAs) to streamline their operations, enhance client service, and scale efficiently. With its cloud-native architecture, TPAs can achieve seamless integration with various data sources and offer a range of customizable tools tailored to meet their specific needs.

#### Key Features

1. **API Access**: Seamless integration with your data sources.
2. **Implementation and Support**: Expert system configuration and guidance.
3. **Reporting and Analytics**:
   - Configurable dashboards.
   - Purpose-built solutions.
4. **Industries**: Empower your organization with solutions for various industries.
5. **Resources**: Access to a resource library, case studies, and events.
6. **About**: Learn more about Origami Risk's support and commitment to resilience.
