### Industries

Origami Risk empowers organizations across various industries to streamline operations and turn data into actionable insights. ### Solutions

- **P&C Insurance**: Cloud-native tools for policy, billing, and claims, providing efficient and secure operations.
- **RMS**: Centralizes and manages insurable risk data, streamlining claims administration and enhancing member engagement.
- **TCOR Analytics**: Claims management and incidents & events management.
- **Insurance Program Management**: Allocations, exposure management.
- **Environment, Health & Safety (EHS)**: Operational risk management.
- **Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)**: Identify, assess, and mitigate enterprise risk.
- **Business Continuity Management**: Enterprise risk management, internal audit, internal controls management, policy & procedure management, and third-party risk management.
- **Healthcare Solutions**: Integrated solutions for healthcare providers.
- **Integrated Risk Management (IRM)**: Unifies risk, safety, and compliance on one platform.
- **Industries**: Specialized solutions tailored to different industries.
