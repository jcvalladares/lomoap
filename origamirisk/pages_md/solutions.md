# Origami Risk: A Comprehensive Platform for Risk and Insurance

## Overview
Origami Risk offers a range of configurable risk and insurance solutions designed to enhance operations and streamline processes. The platform is built for speed, scalability, and security, providing seamless integration with data sources, expert system configuration, and customizable solutions for risk, safety, and insurance.

- **Cloud-native Tools**: Policy, billing, and claims administration.

- **TCOR Analytics**: Claims management, incidents & events, insurance program management, allocations, exposure management.
- **Environment, Health & Safety (EHS)**: Operational risk management.
- **Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)**: Identify, assess, and mitigate enterprise risk.
- **Business Continuity Management**: Enterprise risk management, internal audit, internal controls management, policy & procedure management, third-party risk management.
- **Healthcare Solutions**: Integrated solutions for healthcare providers.
- **Integrated Risk Management (IRM)**: Unify risk, safety, and compliance on one platform.
- **Industries**: Empower your organization with solutions that streamline operations and turn data into actionable insights.
