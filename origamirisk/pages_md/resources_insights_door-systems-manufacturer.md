### Case Study: Door Systems Manufacturer

A leading door systems manufacturer faced challenges managing fragmented safety and risk data across its manufacturing and construction divisions. Using Origami Risk's platform, they unified both risk and safety data and processes. Implementing automated data feeds, anonymous reporting portals, and integrated analytics, the organization achieved significant improvements in incident reporting efficiency and data visibility. The solution transformed safety reporting through streamlined workflows and enabled the company to leverage leading indicators, resulting in a 65% reduction in recordable incident rates, 50% decrease in lost time incidents, and 30% reduction in retained claims costs.
