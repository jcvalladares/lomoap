### Origami Risk Recognized Among Nation's Best Companies to Work For

#### Overview
Origami Risk, a leading risk, safety, and insurance software as a service (SaaS) firm, has been recognized among the nation's best and brightest companies to work for by the National Association of Business Resources (NABR).

#### Key Highlights
- **Recognition**: NABR honored Origami Risk with its "Best and Brightest Companies to Work For" award.
- **Recognition from Inc. Magazine**: Origami Risk also received the 2025 Best Workplaces award from Inc. magazine.
- **Honors**: Origami Risk has received over 50 honors for workplace culture, product innovation, and client service.
- **Commitment to Talent**: The company focuses on hiring and retaining top talent in technology, insurance, and risk management.
- **Collaborative Culture**: Origami Risk fosters a culture of collaboration and support, with employees feeling empowered to do their best work.

#### Impact
The recognition demonstrates Origami Risk's strong commitment to providing high-quality solutions and excellent customer service, aligning with the values of the NABR award. The company aims to continue growing organically while staying focused on listening closely to clients and delivering tailored solutions that help them manage risk and achieve meaningful results.
