# Enduris Claims Administration Transition to Origami Risk

## Introduction
Enduris, a risk sharing pool serving local government districts across Washington state, faced several operational challenges before transitioning to Origami Risk's cloud-based platform in 2017. The company struggled with fragmented legacy systems, manual processes, and inconsistent data management. With Origami Risk's flexible, integrated platform, Enduris was able to streamline workflows, improve data accuracy, and enhance member service.

## Key Features of the Transition
1. **Claims and Policy Administration**: Origami Risk provided a unified platform for claims and policy administration, eliminating the need for multiple disconnected systems.
2. **Self-Serve Configuration**: Users could easily make changes to screen layouts, set up workflows, and create rule-based mail merges.
3. **Document Control**: Better organization and member sharing capabilities ensured that important documents were accessible to members.
4. **Policy and Claims Integration**: A unified system eliminated disconnected processes, reducing errors and improving data accuracy.
5. **Strengthened Workflows and Member Experience**:
   - Streamlined workflows reduced manual processes and improved efficiency.
   - Automated notifications and self-service portals reduced administrative burden on staff.
   - Self-service portals allowed members to access information and documents, reducing administrative costs.
6. **Results and Ongoing Innovation**:
   - Improved operational efficiency and reduced manual processes.
   - Enhanced data accuracy and consistency.
   - Enhanced member service through self-service portals.
   - Configurable flexibility and ease of use.
7. **Future Roadmap**:
   - Implementing mobile forms for risk management reviews and assessments.
   - Developing "What-If" analysis functionality between rating programs.
   - Leveraging APIs to enhance data exchange with their accounting system.
   - Further enhancing their use of worksheets for more automated rating calculations.

## Conclusion
Enduris' successful transition to Origami Risk's platform has delivered numerous benefits, including improved operational efficiency, enhanced data accuracy, and enhanced member service. By leveraging a flexible, integrated platform, Enduris was able to streamline workflows, improve data accuracy, and enhance member service. The company is well-positioned to continue delivering even greater value to its members through enhanced operational efficiencies and self-service capabilities.
