# Healthcare Risk Management Software

## Overview

Origami Risk is a leading healthcare risk management software platform that offers innovative solutions to enhance operations, improve outcomes, and comply with regulatory requirements. Our platform is designed to streamline risk management, patient safety, and quality programs across the healthcare continuum. 

### Key Features

1. **Patient Safety and Quality**: Our platform provides a comprehensive set of tools to improve care outcomes, foster a culture of safety, and ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.
   - **Claims Management and Administration**: Streamlines healthcare claims from FNOL to closure, consolidates data, and optimizes workflow.
   - **Provider Performance**: Supports excellence in clinical practice, measures performance, and ensures compliance with organizational and industry standards.
   - **Employee Health and Safety**: Manages testing and screening, vaccination records, employee certifications, and more with tools that streamline tracking, documentation, and reporting.

2. **Environment, Health and Safety (EHS)**: Promotes a safer care environment by managing hazards, tracking events, and ensuring compliance with environmental and occupational health standards.
   - **Operational Risk Management**: Identifies, assesses, and mitigates enterprise risk.
   - **Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC)**: Power enterprise risk management, compliance, and vendor risk management programs that minimize risk, improve outcomes, and contribute to operational resilience.

3. **Case Studies**: Explore our clients' real-world success stories and digitizing safety to transform incident management with Origami Risk.

### Integrations

Our platform integrates seamlessly with various data sources, enabling organizations to gain a holistic view of their risk landscape. This allows for more effective risk management and improved decision-making.
