# Third-Party Administrators (TPAs)

## Overview

Origami Risk is a cloud-native platform designed to streamline claims administration, enhance client service, and scale operations. With its flexible tools, TPAs can achieve faster, more accurate claims management while delivering transparency and strengthening relationships with every client they serve.

### Key Features

1. **Comprehensive Tools**: Our platform provides a range of tools for automating claims workflows, advanced reporting, and client-branded portals.
2. **Intuitive App**: An intuitive mobile app allows users to report claims on-the-go.
3. **Configurable Dashboards**: Users can configure dashboards to monitor key performance indicators.
4. **Purpose-built Solutions**: Solutions tailored to improve outcomes for risk, safety, and insurance.
5. **Centralized Data Management**: Centralizes insurable risk data for efficient management.
6. **Powerful Safety Culture**: Enables a strong safety culture through operational risk management.
7. **Enterprise Risk Management**: Identifies, assesses, and mitigates enterprise risk.
8. **Business Continuity Management**: Manages internal audit, internal controls, policy & procedure management.
9. **Integrated Solutions**: Offers integrated solutions for healthcare providers, construction, government, healthcare, manufacturing, transportation, and resource management.
