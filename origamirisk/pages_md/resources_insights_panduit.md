# Panduit GRC

## Introduction
Panduit's Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) solution streamlines processes to enhance buy-in from risk owners for network infrastructure and industrial electrical wiring company, Panduit. The team led by Mark Beres and Stan Szudrowicz started the ERM program in 2018 and has since refined it over the course of three months of use.

## Solution Overview
Origami Risk provides a flexible ERM solution that is adaptable to Panduit's unique hybrid framework and scoring models. The system includes automated workflows, notifications, and an easy-to-use data import tool. The team has successfully incorporated risk metrics into risk owner's dashboards, which allows for quick reviews and adjustments.

## Benefits
- **Enhanced Risk Ownership**: Data in a single system reduces barriers to having risk owners "on the risk."
- **Expanded Self-Service Options**: Risk owners can now log in to the system to provide details about what has changed about a risk over the past month or quarter.
- **Reinforced Process Consistency**: Use of Origami Risk helps the Panduit risk management team reinforce the consistent execution of ERM program processes.

## Future Plans
The team will continue to refine processes and build upon the successes of the company's ERM program. Origami Risk is playing an important part in the program's maturation and evolution. In the near term, the risk management team will incorporate risk metrics into risk owner's dashboards, which will allow for values to be quickly reviewed and, if necessary, adjusted. The team will also be adding detailed action plans to the system. These will allow risk owners to quickly see where they stand in relation to defined deadlines and milestones, and give the team the ability to report on the status of action plans across the business.

## Conclusion
Panduit's GRC solution has streamlined processes and improved accountability for risk owners, leading to increased trust and alignment with the company's risk culture. With support from stakeholders, the Panduit team continues to refine processes and build upon the successes of the company's ERM program.
