Learn more Leadership Careers News Partners Contact Us Contact us Log in Request a demo Insights / Press Release Origami Risk Recognized Among Leading Third-Party Risk Management Software Providers by Independent Analyst Firm July 23, 2025 EHS GRC Integrated Risk Management (IRM) RMIS Platform Share Single platform for TPRM, GRC, RMIS and EHS strengthens risk and compliance CHICAGO–( BUSINESS WIRE )–Origami Risk, the industry-leading risk, safety and insurance Software as a Service (SaaS) technology firm, has been recognized in the 2025 Verdantix Smart Innovators: Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) report of 21 leading TPRM solutions. According to Verdantix, “Risk executives, IT security, compliance and procurement teams should use this report to compare the relative capabilities of vendors across five key functionality areas to inform their TPRM software purchasing decisions for 2025.” Origami delivers its TPRM solution on a single platform along with its established and widely popular functionality for GRC, RMIS, and EHS. This results in fewer silos, stronger connections across risk domains, and the economies and efficiencies of having one provider supporting an entire risk and compliance program. “In 2025, third-party risk has emerged as a strategic priority, as firms grapple with a volatile regulatory landscape, rising cyber threats and the growing complexity of global supplier ecosystems,” commented Christine O’Donnell, Senior Analyst at Verdantix. “Organizations are actively seeking agile platforms that combine real-time visibility, configurable workflows and end-to-end vendor lifecycle management. Providers such as Origami Risk are evolving in response of this shift, offering dynamic risk assessments, vendor portals, incident tracking, compliance workflows and integrated reporting, all of which are critical capabilities in today’s high-stakes risk environment.” “As supply chains evolve, become more complex and are subject to increasing regulatory scrutiny, having a strong TPRM program is essential for our clients to protect operational resilience, data security, and brand reputation,” said Jon Schulz, Senior Market Strategy Lead for GRC at Origami Risk. “The recognition we’ve received from Verdantix validates the strength and maturity of our TPRM capabilities as we continue to work closely with clients on innovative tools and resources to manage this dynamic exposure.” Founded in 2007, Verdantix is an independent research and advisory firm that acts as an essential thought-leader for world-enhancing innovation. In compiling the TPRM report, Verdantix “reviewed the capabilities of over 150 vendors to produce a shortlist of 21 TPRM software providers targeted to the needs of risk professionals.” Vendors included in the report were required to satisfy the following criteria: Possess functionality across all five core capability categories, including third-party life cycle management, risk assessment and due diligence, monitoring and performance management, issue management and remediation, and reporting compliance and analytics. Demonstrate clear risk management coverage across seven of eight risk domains – operational risk, compliance risk, data privacy risk, cyber risk, geopolitical risk, reputational risk, sustainability/ESG risk and financial risk. Retain 125 employees or more. Origami Risk delivers its highly configurable solutions for TPRM, GRC, RMIS and EHS from a secure, scalable platform that includes tools for centralizing data, automating critical workflows, and providing insights into risk and safety initiatives. About Origami Risk Origami Risk empowers leaders in insurance, risk, and safety with a purpose-built, cloud-native platform that optimizes workflows for better data, better insights, and better collaboration. Through highly configurable solutions integrated on a single platform, Origami Risk supports the management of the full lifecycle of risk, from prevention to recovery — helping the experts reduce harm and loss, and respond more rapidly and effectively when it happens. Grounded in continuous innovation and a foundational focus on client success, Origami Risk is trusted by leading organizations to enable greater resilience as they build for the future.
