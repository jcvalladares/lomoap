Learn more Leadership Careers News Partners Contact Us Contact us Log in Request a demo Insights / Press Release Origami Risk Recognized in the 2025 Gartner® Market Guide for Environmental, Health and Safety Software May 29, 2025 EHS Platform Share CHICAGO, May 29, 2025 – Origami Risk, the industry-leading risk, safety and insurance Software as a Service (SaaS) technology firm, has been recognized in the 2025 Gartner® Market Guide for EHS Software. This marks the third consecutive year Origami has been recognized in the guide as a Representative Vendor for its environmental, health and safety (EHS) platform. According to Gartner, “Enterprises invest in environmental, health and safety software to mitigate the environmental and social impacts of their operations and improve EHS performance. CIOs who buy and implement EHS software can use this Market Guide to gain insight into the market.” “Origami’s single platform for EHS, GRC and RMIS enables safety and risk professionals at Fortune 500 and other organizations in multiple industries to apply integrated risk management to enhance safety culture, reduce incidents and claims, and send employees home safely every day,” said Sean Salvas, Senior Market Strategy Lead of Origami’s Risk Solutions division. “We believe that our focus on continuous innovation driven by input from our clients has led to our inclusion in the Gartner report and consistently high ratings by other prominent EHS analysts, while enhancing our standing as a leader in EHS technology.” Origami offers an extensive suite of digital EHS solutions available on a highly-configurable, fully-integrated web and mobile platform. Functionality includes Safety Management, PSM (Process Safety Management), Occupational Health, Chemical Management, and Environmental and Regulatory Compliance. More information on Origami’s EHS suite is available here . Gartner subscribers can access the 2025 Market Guide for EHS Software by visiting gartner.com .
