Learn more Leadership Careers News Partners Contact Us Contact us Log in Request a demo RMIS | CLAIMS MANAGEMENT & CLAIMS ADMINISTRATION Streamline oversight and reduce claims costs Consolidate data, automate workflows, and improve outcomes with tools for managing and administering claims across all lines of coverage. Centralize your claims data and workflows to drive better outcomes Whether your team self-administers or you’re looking to improve how you manage and report on data from TPAs and carriers, Origami Risk delivers comprehensive solutions that can be configured to meet the needs of your claims program. Improve efficiency, enhance visibility, and inform decisions that reduce your total cost of risk with automated workflows, centralized communications and documentation, and intuitive reports and dashboards. CLAIMS MANAGEMENT OFFERINGS Tools to improve claims oversight and outcomes Claims Financials Tracking Monitor reserves, payments, and financial trends to improve forecasting and reduce claims costs. Subrogation & Recovery Tracking Identify recovery opportunities and track subrogation efforts to maximize reimbursements and reduce net loss. Claim Litigation Management Centralize legal documentation and track litigation status to improve collaboration and oversight and reduce legal exposure. Leave of Absence Management Coordinate leave tracking with claims data to ensure compliance and streamline employee communications. Lost Time Tracking Monitor lost time trends and durations to support return-to-work strategies and reduce claim costs Electronic OSHA Reporting Automate OSHA reporting with built-in tools that simplify compliance and reduce administrative burden. Return-to-Work Management Support recovery and reintegration with tools that track modified duty, restrictions, and progress toward full return. Claim Benchmarking Compare performance across claims, adjusters, and programs to identify trends and improve outcomes. Claim Audits Conduct internal or external audits with configurable templates and workflows to ensure accuracy and compliance. Adjuster Performance Analysis Evaluate adjuster effectiveness using performance metrics to improve accountability and claim outcomes. CLAIMS ADMINISTRATION OFFERINGS Configurable tools for in-house claims handling Payment Processing Automate and track payments to ensure accuracy, timeliness, and compliance with internal and external requirements. Reserve Management Set and adjust reserves with audit trails and approval workflows to maintain financial accuracy and transparency. OFAC Sanctions Search Screen payees against Office of Foreign Assets Controls (OFAC) lists to ensure compliance with federal sanctions regulations. 1099 Production Support Generate and manage 1099 forms for claim-related payments to support year-end tax reporting. ISO Claims Search Search ISO databases to identify duplicate claims and uncover potential fraud or prior incidents. CMS-111 Reporting Automate CMS-111 submissions to ensure compliance with Medicare reporting requirements. Official Disability Guidelines (ODG) Integration Access evidence-based treatment guidelines to support claim decisions and improve return-to-work outcomes. State Forms Generate and submit up-to-date, jurisdiction-specific forms to meet regulatory requirements across states. Indemnity Benefit Calculations Calculate indemnity payments with built-in formulas and rules to ensure accuracy and compliance. Electronic Injury Reporting (EDI – FROI/SROI) Submit First and Subsequent Reports of Injury electronically to streamline compliance and reduce delays.
