Origami’s Policy & Procedure Management solution streamlines creation, review, and distribution on one platform. Automated workflows and attestation tracking reduce manual effort, improve visibility, and help compliance leaders demonstrate readiness during audits. Connect policies to risks, controls, and more to reinforce alignment across your broader risk management program. OFFERINGS Tools for managing your organization’s policies — from draft to attestation Policy Management Draft, edit, and approve policy documents using configurable templates and workflows. Maintain version control and audit trails to ensure transparency. Issues Management Identify gaps in your policy program and assign action plans. Use insights from past events to improve future compliance and readiness. Policy Repository Store and organize policies in a centralized, searchable database. Role-based access ensures employees and contractors see only the latest approved versions. Attestation Collect and track employee attestations with automated reminders and real-time reporting. Link attestations to policies for a complete audit-ready record.
