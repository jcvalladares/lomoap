Origami Risk Recognized in the 2025 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Governance, Risk and Compliance Tools, Assurance Leaders Read more Minimize disruption with proactive planning and response Strengthen readiness and reduce downtime by supporting every phase of your business continuity program — from identifying critical processes and assessing risk to coordinating response and accelerating recovery. Origami Risk’s Business Continuity Management solution centralizes planning, automates updates, and connects continuity efforts to assets, locations, and compliance requirements. OFFERINGS Coordinate continuity efforts with connected tools Continuity Planning Build and maintain continuity plans that reflect your organization’s structure. Map plans to locations, processes, and compliance needs, assign ownership, and automate updates to keep content current and actionable. Crisis Response Prepare for and manage crisis events using task tracking, communication tools, and scenario-based planning. Strengthen coordination and reduce response time across departments. Disaster Response Launch incident management workflows with automated notifications and step-by-step task execution. Monitor progress and align actions to recovery time objectives (RTOs) for faster restoration of operations. Issue Management Identify and resolve gaps in your continuity program. Capture issues, assign action plans, and use insights from past events to improve future preparedness and response.
