Learn more Leadership Careers News Partners Contact Us Contact us Log in Request a demo Insights / Press Release Origami Risk Recognized in the 2025 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for SaaS P&C Core Platforms, North America September 23, 2025 Insurance Solution Insurance Platform Share Only Vendor in Report Advancing to Higher Category CHICAGO–( BUSINESS WIRE )–Origami Risk, the industry-leading risk, safety and insurance Software as a Service (SaaS) technology firm, has been recognized in the 2025 Gartner® Magic Quadrant SaaS P&C Core Platforms, North America . “We’re excited about Origami’s elevated positioning in the Gartner report this year along with the fact that our core platform continues to earn high marks from numerous widely respected industry analysts,” said Earne Bentley, Chief Revenue Officer, Origami Risk. “At the same time, the sustained growth and ongoing success of our dynamic core solution set underscore our strong market traction and incredible momentum.” Origami Risk offers a full suite of end-to-end, integrated SaaS solutions for policy, rating, billing, loss control, claims administration, reporting and analytics. It also offers a full-service ISO and NCCI bureau content management solution and a comprehensive digital engagement experience for all internal and external stakeholders. Gartner subscribers can access the 2025 GartnerⓇ Magic Quadrant for SaaS P&C Core Platforms, North America by visiting gartner.com .
