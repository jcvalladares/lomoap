Learn more Leadership Careers News Partners Contact Us Contact us Log in Request a demo Insights / Case Study Mission Underwriters Policy Administration Insurance Download case study (143KB) Share This case study explores how Mission Underwriters implemented Origami Risk’s cloud-based Policy Administration and Billing solutions to enable rapid program launches and operational growth. By leveraging a flexible, integrated policy administration system, Mission Underwriters achieved impressive speed-to-market capabilities while maintaining data integrity across diverse insurance programs. The platform’s unified approach to data governance has allowed program administrators to closely monitor performance and make data-driven decisions. Since implementation, Mission has launched their first program series in just 90 days, with some new series launching in as few as 60 days. The company has signed nearly 30 program series, implemented almost 60 diverse lines of business, and dramatically exceeded financial targets — achieving $32 million in GWP in their first year (88% above target) and reaching over $500 million in 2024.
