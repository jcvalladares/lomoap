### Title: Join the THREE Partner Agency Program

#### Summary:
Join the THREE partner agency program at Berkshire Hathaway to provide comprehensive insurance to small business owners. With our unique all-in-one business insurance policy, you can enjoy a single comprehensive policy eliminating coverage gaps, one monthly bill, and a single number to call for filing a claim. Our dedicated Agency Service Team provides assistance to both policyholders and agents, access to the THREE platform allows you to quote, bind, and issue a policy online, and we offer flexible and customizable coverage options to meet your needs. Whether you're a restaurant, bar, bakeries, cafes, food trucks, artisan/trade contractors, hotels, motels, inns, bed & breakfasts, or healthcare providers, THREE offers tailored solutions to ensure your business is fully protected. Ready to start working with us? Apply today!
