## Title: Comprehensive Coverage for Creative Services Businesses

### Summary:
Three by Berkshire Hathaway offers comprehensive coverage for creative services businesses, covering operations, people, property, and technology. With a focus on protecting against risks such as cyber incidents, copyright infringement, spyware, errors, workplace injuries, discrimination, property damage, and smoke damage, the service helps small businesses grow and succeed. 

### Key Features:
- **Operations Coverage**: Protects against cyber incidents, copyright infringement, spyware, and errors.
- **People Coverage**: Covers workplace injuries, discrimination, and property damage.
- **Property Coverage**: Protects against fire, water, smoke damage, theft, and property damage.
- **Technology Coverage**: Provides comprehensive coverage for digital marketing, media, and social media.
