# Errors & Omissions Insurance by Berkshire Hathaway

## Overview

Three by Berkshire Hathaway offers comprehensive coverage for errors and omissions, including directors and officers, liquor liability, general liability, employment practices liability, fiduciary liability, property fire, theft, flood & water damage, wind & hail, earthquake, equipment breakdown, inland marine business, auto collision, comprehensive auto liability, cargo liability, hired & non-owned business interruption, business income, extra expenses, cyber data breach, and workers' compensation. This coverage is included in every Three policy.

## Key Features

- **Covered Risks**: Includes all listed above.
- **Single Policy Coverage**: No need for multiple policies; one comprehensive policy covers all needs.
- **Policy Duration**: Available from January 1, 2019 to December 31, 2025.
