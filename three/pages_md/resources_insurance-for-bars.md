# Insurance for Bars by Berkshire Hathaway

## Summary
Insurance is essential for small business owners operating bars and taverns. This guide covers various types of insurance needed, including liquor liability, employment practices liability, commercial property, cyber liability, and workers' compensation. The guide also provides tips for assessing and updating your insurance coverage.

## Contents
- **What Type of Insurance Do You Need for a Bar or Tavern?**
  - Employees
  - Physical Space
  - Customer Data and Sensitive Information

- **Liquor Liability Insurance**
  - Protecting Your Business Against Injuries Caused by Patrons Who Were Served After Leaving

- **Employment Practices Liability Insurance**
  - Covering Allegations of Sexual Harassment, Wrongful Termination, and Discrimination

- **Commercial Property Insurance**
  - Protecting the Physical Items Your Business Owns or Rents

- **Cyber Liability Insurance**
  - Protecting Against Stolen Customer Data, Ransomware Attacks, and Compromised Software

- **Workers' Compensation**
  - Helping Protect Your Employees and Your Business in the Event of a Work-Related Injury or Illness
