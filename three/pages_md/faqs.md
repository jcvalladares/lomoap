# Three by Berkshire Hathaway

## Overview

Three by Berkshire Hathaway offers comprehensive insurance coverage through a simple, straightforward policy, making it easier for small businesses to protect themselves. With its broad coverage and streamlined process, it provides peace of mind for entrepreneurs and small businesses alike.

## Key Features

- **Comprehensive Coverage**: Three by Berkshire Hathaway covers a wide range of risks, including property, liability, workers' compensation, and more.
- **Simple Policy**: The policy is designed to be easy to understand and manage, allowing businesses to focus on running their operations.
- **No Hidden Gaps**: Unlike other small business insurers, Three by Berkshire Hathaway ensures that all necessary coverage is included in the policy, eliminating the need for additional purchases.
- **New Policy Design**: The policy is new, which makes it difficult to compare with existing policies, but our Small Business Advisors have been trained to decipher and compare insurance policies.
- **Easy to Compare**: Gathering current policies and calling 1-800-507-4495 allows users to easily compare their coverage with what they currently have.
