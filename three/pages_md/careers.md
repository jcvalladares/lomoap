# Three by Berkshire Hathaway

## Overview
Three by Berkshire Hathaway is a small business insurance company that offers comprehensive coverage for various industries including cleaning and janitorial services, hospitality, residential construction, restaurants and taverns, technology services, trade contractors, resources, articles and whitepapers, FAQs, certificates, claims, claims process, file a worker's compensation claim, file a property, auto, or liability claim, and more. The company provides its customers with access to the latest updates, subscription options, contact information for small business advisors, and social media profiles where they can stay informed about their insurance needs.

## Job Openings
### Senior Data Scientist - Portfolio Strategy
This role is part of a small team responsible for building out THREE's analytical foundation to help shape data-driven business decisions across marketing, underwriting, operations, and more. The successful candidate will have experience working with large datasets and statistical analysis tools, as well as strong communication skills to explain complex data insights to non-technical stakeholders.

### Remote - USA
This role is available remotely for US-based employees. The responsibilities include developing and implementing analytics solutions, conducting market research, and providing recommendations to improve business processes. The candidate should be proficient in programming languages such as Python and SQL, as well as experience with machine learning frameworks like TensorFlow or PyTorch.
