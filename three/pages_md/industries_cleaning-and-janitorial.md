# Cleaning & Janitorial Insurance: Comprehensive Coverage for Your Business

## Summary
Three by Berkshire Hathaway offers comprehensive cleaning and janitorial insurance, providing workers' compensation, liability, property, auto, and cyber liability coverage to protect businesses of all sizes. The policy is designed to grow with your business and offers flexible options to expand coverage as your needs evolve.

## Key Features
- **Workers' Compensation**: Covers medical bills and lost wages.
- **Liability**: Provides protection against customer property damage, employee practices, and errors and omissions.
- **Property**: Protects the physical items your business owns.
- **Auto**: Covers towing reimbursements, accident liability, and collision damage.
- **Cyber Liability**: Automatically included in the policy, covering expenses of data breaches or other cyberattacks.

## Benefits
- **Affordable**: Offers comprehensive coverage at reasonable rates.
- **Comprehensive**: Covers all necessary risks specific to the janitorial and cleaning industry.
- **Flexible**: Can be expanded as your business grows.
