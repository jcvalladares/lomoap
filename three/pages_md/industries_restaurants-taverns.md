## Overview
Three by Berkshire Hathaway provides comprehensive insurance for restaurant businesses, protecting your employees, property, and operations from unique risks such as workers' compensation, property insurance, and cyber liability. With its all-in-one coverage, Restaurant Business Insurance helps ensure that your business remains operational and profitable.

## Key Features
- **Workers' Compensation**: Ensures that your employees are protected from injuries and illnesses caused by their work.
- **Commercial Auto Insurance**: Protects your restaurant from accidents involving vehicles.
- **Damage to Own Property**: Covers the cost of repairs and replacement for damage to your restaurant premises.
- **Professional Mistakes**: Provides coverage for errors made by your staff, helping you defend your business against lawsuits.
