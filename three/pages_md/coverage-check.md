### Insurance Coverage Check-Up

#### Overview
Use this self-assessment tool to assess your current insurance coverage and identify areas for improvement. It helps you understand which industries your business operates in and what types of coverage you have.

#### How to Use
1. **Fill Out Your Information**: Enter your name, preferred method of communication, and select your business industry.
2. **Answer Questions**: Answer each question to determine if you need additional coverage.
3. **Select Coverages**: Mark the boxes for the coverages that apply to your business.
4. **Get Results**: Click "GET RESULTS" to see your coverage summary and recommendations.

#### Additional Policies
Some coverages may have been purchased as individual policies or included in larger policies. Review the list of core policies and select all that apply to your business.
