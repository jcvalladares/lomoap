# Berkshire Hathaway Three

## Overview

Three is a diversified insurance company known for its expertise in various industries including cleaning, janitorial, hospitality, residential construction, restaurants, taverns, technology services, trade contractors, resources, articles, whitepapers, FAQs, certificates, claims, claims process, file a worker's compensation claim, file a property, auto, or liability claim, and more. The company also provides agents with quotes and services.

### Key Features

- **Diverse Industry Expertise**: Three offers coverage across multiple sectors.
- **Quick Access to Quotes**: Customers can easily obtain quotes via phone or online.
- **Customer Support**: Offers contact options for small business advisors.
- **Company Information**: Provides details about the company's history, policies, and locations.

### Get Quote

To obtain a quote, customers need to fill out an online form. They will be asked to provide their email address, full name, business location, number of employees, and their primary industry. After submitting the form, they will be notified via email of their updated quote.

### Contact Us

For any inquiries, customers can reach out to the company directly by calling 1-800-507-4495. They can also visit the company's website or social media platforms to find additional ways to connect with them.

---

### Small Business Advisor

If customers are interested in consulting with a small business advisor, they can call 1-800-507-4495. This service is available Monday through Friday, 8 a m to 9 p m Eastern Standard Time (EST).
