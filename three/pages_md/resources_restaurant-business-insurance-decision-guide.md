- **Workers' Compensation Insurance**: Covers injuries and illnesses caused by work.
- **Employment Practices Liability Insurance (EPLI)**: Covers lawsuits related to workplace issues.
- **Property Damage Insurance**: Covers damage to buildings, vehicles, and perishable food items.
