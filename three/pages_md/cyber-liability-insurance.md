## Cyber Liability Insurance - THREE

### Overview
Three by Berkshire Hathaway offers comprehensive cyber liability coverage designed to protect businesses against various forms of cyber risks, including data breaches, hacks, and fraudulent activity. The policy covers both data breach and cyber attack costs, helping businesses recover lost data and notify customers, while also providing coverage for business interruption, repair & recovery, and specialized coverages like those required by specific industries.

### Why Do I Need Cyber Liability Coverage?
The increased online presence of businesses creates a significant risk of cyber attacks, such as phishing or ransomware attacks, stolen customer data, or compromised software or hacks. A THREE policy automatically includes cyber coverage to protect businesses against these risks.

### Key Benefits
- **Data Breach and Cyber Attack Costs**: Protects businesses from the cost of legal fees, data recovery, notifying customers, and business interruption.
- **Business Interruption**: Helps pay for lost net income due to a cyber attack.
- **Repair & Recovery**: Pays to repair or replace damaged systems and data.
- **Specialized Coverages**: Provides protection for unique risks specific to each industry, such as accidental errors and omissions, wine bar liability, and trucker cargo.
