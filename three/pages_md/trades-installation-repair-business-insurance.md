# Berkshire Hathaway Three Comprehensive Insurance

## Overview
Three by Berkshire Hathaway is a comprehensive insurance solution designed specifically for small businesses that offer trades, installation, and repair services. The policy provides comprehensive coverage for various risks associated with these industries, including business liability, errors and omissions, directors and officers, general liability, employment practices liability, fiduciary liability, property damage, theft, flood, water damage, wind, hail, earthquake, equipment breakdown, inland marine business, auto collision, comprehensive auto liability, hired and non-owned business interruption, business income, extra expenses, cyber data breach, and workers' compensation.
