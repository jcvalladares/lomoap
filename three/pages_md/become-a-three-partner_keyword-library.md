## Keyword Library

### Summary
The Keyword Library provides an overview of eligible after-market auto parts stores, wholesalers, and dealerships. It includes categories like "No Repair or Service," "Aftermarket Auto Parts Store," "Auto Dealership (New and Used)," etc., with options for retail, wholesale, and new car dealerships. The library also offers search functionality on the right side of the table.

### Headings
- **Keyword Eligible Aftermarket Auto Parts Store**: Provides information about after-market auto parts stores that offer no repairs or services.
- **Aftermarket Auto Parts Wholesalers**: Lists wholesalers offering after-market auto parts at various prices.
- **Antique Auto Sales**: Specifies antique auto sales locations.
- **Auto Dealership (New and Used)**: Includes details on used and new car dealerships.
- **Auto Dealership (New)**: Offers information specifically for new car dealerships.
- **Auto Dealership (Used)**: Specifies dealerships that sell used cars.
- **Auto Parts Wholesaler**: Lists wholesalers selling auto parts.
- **Auto Rental Shop**: Describes rental shops providing auto transportation.
- **Auto Sales**: Indicates auto sales locations.
- **Automobile Parts and Accessories Store**: Lists automobile parts and accessories stores.
- **Retail**: Specifies retail locations for automotive products.
- **Car Batteries Store**: Provides information about car batteries.
- **Car Rentals and Leasing**: Includes details on car rentals and leasing services.
- **Car Tire Dealer**: Specifies tire dealerships.
- **Gas Station**: Lists gas stations offering vehicle maintenance and fueling services.
- **Landlord: Residential Over 4 Family**: Indicates landlord locations where residents over 4 families can rent apartments.
- **Motorcycle Dealer**: Lists motorcycle dealerships.
- **Motorcycle Dealership**: Specifies motorcycle dealerships.
- **New Car Dealership**: Provides information specifically for new car dealerships.
- **Outdoor Recreational Dealer**: Lists outdoor recreational dealer locations.
- **Rows per page**: Displays the number of rows per page.
- **Previous page**: Allows users to navigate through previous pages.
- **Next page**: Enables users to move to the next page.
