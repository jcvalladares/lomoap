# General Liability Business Insurance - THREE

## Overview

Three by Berkshire Hathaway offers comprehensive coverage for small businesses across various industries, including cleaning & janitorial, hospitality, residential construction, restaurants & taverns, technology services, trade contractors, and resources. The policy includes broad protection against different types of liability demands, covering potential gaps in protection and eliminating unnecessary complexity.

## Key Features

- **Comprehensive Coverage**: Protects against a wide range of liabilities, including property damage, bodily injury, advertising injury, and more.
- **One-Policy Solution**: Offers one comprehensive policy that covers your entire business - operations, people, and property.
- **Flexibility**: Provides options for specialized coverages tailored to specific industry needs.
- **Easy Quote Process**: Quickly obtain a quote over the phone or save information for future use.
- **Customer Support**: Offers professional service and excellent customer support.

## Benefits

- **Cost Savings**: Reduced risk of unexpected gaps in coverage or overlapping coverage.
- **Simplified Accounting**: Better management of multiple policies with a single comprehensive policy.
- **Professional Assistance**: Expert guidance from experienced saleswomen to ensure accurate quotes and understand your needs.

## Comparison with Current Policies

### Business Owner’s Policy (BOP)

- **Coverage Limitations**: Limited coverage compared to a comprehensive policy, focusing primarily on property and liability.
- **Complexity**: Requires careful examination of fine print to avoid unintended gaps in coverage or costly overlaps.
- **Cost**: Generally higher than a comprehensive policy due to the limited scope.

### Comparison with Three by Berkshire Hathaway

- **Broad Coverage**: Covers a far broader array of risks compared to a BOP, reducing the risk of unexpected gaps or coverage overlaps.
- **Simple Pricing**: Lower cost than other providers due to the streamlined policy structure.
- **Ease of Use**: Quick quote process and easy comparison with other policies.

## Conclusion

Three by Berkshire Hathaway offers comprehensive coverage for small businesses, providing broad protection against different types of liability demands. Its one-policy solution, flexibility, and easy quote process make it an attractive option for businesses seeking reliable insurance coverage. Whether you need specialized coverages or want a comprehensive solution, Three by Berkshire Hathaway offers the best choice for your business needs.
