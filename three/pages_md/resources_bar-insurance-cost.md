## What Does Bar Insurance Cost?

### Summary

Bar insurance is crucial for protecting businesses that serve alcohol, covering various potential liabilities and risks. It provides financial security and legal protection against unexpected events such as lawsuits, property damage, or employee injuries. Understanding the different types of coverage offered by bar insurance from THREE and how to calculate costs can help owners manage their operations effectively.

### Key Points

- **Liquor Liability Insurance**: Protects businesses from legal claims related to serving alcohol.
- **Property Insurance**: Covers damage to the building and its contents.
- **Workers' Compensation Insurance**: Protects employees from workplace injuries.
- **Commercial Auto Insurance**: Provides coverage for vehicle-related risks.
- **Employment Practices Liability Insurance**: Protects against employee misconduct.
- **Comprehensive Coverage**: Includes all necessary insurance types tailored to bar operations.
