# Workers Compensation Coverage - THREE by Berkshire Hathaway

## Summary
Berkshire Hathaway offers workers compensation coverage through their "THREE" policy. This comprehensive plan includes errors & omissions, directors & officers, liquor liability, general liability, employment practices liability, fiduciary liability, property fire, theft, flood & water damage, wind & hail, earthquake, equipment breakdown, inland marine business, auto collision, comprehensive auto liability, cargo liability, hired & non-owned business interruption, business income, extra expenses, cyber data breach, and cyber liability.

## Key Features
- **Error & Omissions**: Protects against intentional wrongdoings.
- **Directors & Officers**: Ensures protection against professional misconduct.
- **Liquor Liability**: Covers losses from illegal drinking and serving alcohol.
- **General Liability**: Protects against all types of physical harm caused by negligence.
- **Employment Practices Liability**: Protects employees from wrongful termination or discrimination.
- **Fiduciary Liability**: Protects individuals who make decisions on behalf of others.
- **Property Fire, Theft, Flood & Water Damage**: Provides coverage for property damage.
- **Wind & Hail**: Covers damage from severe weather events.
- **Earthquake**: Protects against earthquakes.
- **Equipment Breakdown**: Provides coverage for machinery damage.
- **Inland Marine Business**: Protects against maritime accidents.
- **Auto Collision**: Protects against collisions between vehicles.
- **Comprehensive Auto Liability**: Provides comprehensive coverage for vehicle damages.
- **Cargo Liability**: Protects against damage to goods during transportation.
- **Hired & Non-Owned Business Interruption**: Protects against interruptions in business operations.
- **Business Income**: Provides coverage for lost revenue due to business disruptions.
- **Extra Expenses**: Provides coverage for additional costs incurred due to business disruptions.
- **Cyber Data Breach**: Provides coverage for damage to personal data.
- **Cyber Liability**: Provides coverage for cyber attacks and data breaches.
