### General Liability Coverage - THREE by Berkshire Hathaway

#### Summary
General liability coverage is included in every THREE policy. The company provides comprehensive coverage for various business-related risks, including errors and omissions, directors and officers, liquor liability, employment practices liability, fiduciary liability, property fire, theft, flood, water damage, wind, hail, earthquake, equipment breakdown, inland marine business, auto collision, comprehensive auto liability, cargo liability, hired and non-owned business interruption, business income, extra expenses, cyber data breach, and workers' compensation. The policy starts on the selected business location and includes a free quote option available online. Customers can contact a Small Business Advisor at 1-800-507-4495 or call them directly at 1-800-507-4495 for assistance.
