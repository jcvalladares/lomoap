### Title: Comprehensive Insurance for Your Small Business

#### Summary:
Three by Berkshire Hathaway offers a comprehensive yet uncomplicated policy designed specifically for small businesses. This policy covers theft of and physical damage to property owned or leased, along with liability and workers' compensation, providing a single solution for all critical business needs. Unlike competitors, Three removes fine print and gotchas, offering a straightforward, hassle-free experience. Whether you're in the hospitality, residential construction, or technology services industry, Three has got you covered.
