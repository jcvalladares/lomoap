## Three by Berkshire Hathaway

### Overview
Three by Berkshire Hathaway is a comprehensive insurance solution designed specifically for accounting businesses. The platform offers various types of liability protection, including errors and omissions, directors and officers, general liability, employment practices liability, fiduciary liability, property damage, theft, flood and water damage, wind and hail, earthquake, equipment breakdown, inland marine business, auto collision, comprehensive auto liability, hired and non-owned business interruption, business income, extra expenses, cyber data breach, cyber liability, and workers' compensation.

### Key Features
- **Comprehensive Coverage**: One policy covers all necessary liabilities.
- **No Multiple Policies**: Reduces costs and simplifies management.
- **Multi-State Coverage**: Available in multiple states.
- **Online Quote Request**: Easy access to get a quote quickly.
- **Direct Agent Service**: Accessible through agents or over the phone.
