# Berkshire Hathaway THREE

## Overview
Berkshire Hathaway's Three is a comprehensive insurance solution designed for wealth management businesses. It offers a range of coverage options tailored to meet various needs, including liability errors and omissions, directors and officers, general liability, employment practices liability, fiduciary liability, property fire theft, flood and water damage, wind and hail, earthquake, equipment breakdown, inland marine business, auto collision, comprehensive auto liability, hired and non-owned business interruption, business income, extra expenses, cyber data breach, cyber liability, workers' compensation, lost wages, medical treatment, and more.
