## How to Get a Certificate of Insurance

### Introduction
A Certificate of Insurance (COI) is a document that confirms your company's insurance coverage, especially for business relationships. It serves as proof of insurance and demonstrates that you're a responsible business owner. This article explains how to obtain a COI from THREE, the benefits of having one, and tips for managing your COIs effectively.

### Key Points
1. **Definition**: A COI is another name for a proof of insurance document.
2. **Purpose**: COIs are essential for confirming insurance coverage to business partners and other decision-makers.
3. **Types of Coverage**: Includes various types of insurance such as Business Liability, Workers' Compensation, and more.
4. **Policy Limits**: Specifies the maximum amount the insurer will pay for a covered claim.
5. **Additional Insured**: Lists third parties that may be entitled to coverage.
6. **Requesting a COI**: Visit the Certificate of Insurance page on THREE's website and submit a request.
7. **Reviewing and Verifying the COI**: Carefully read the COI to ensure accuracy and completeness.
8. **Providing the COI**: Once verified, provide it to the requesting party.
9. **Cost**: Free COIs are provided by THREE for freelancers and independent contractors.
10. **Additional Insured**: Adding an additional insured on the COI is useful for special events and venues.
11. **Managing COIs**: Keep COIs organized, review regularly, and stay in touch with your provider.
12. **Certification**: Share your proof of insurance with clients and partners.

### Conclusion
Having a Certificate of Insurance is crucial for demonstrating financial responsibility and building trust with others. By following these steps and tips, you can ensure that your company is always prepared to demonstrate its commitment to risk management and seize opportunities without unnecessary delays.
