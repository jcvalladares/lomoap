# Three by Berkshire Hathaway: Comprehensive Trade Contractor Insurance

## Overview
Three by Berkshire Hathaway is a leading provider of comprehensive contractor business insurance that offers a range of essential protections for trade contractors. From Workers' Compensation to Professional Liability, Property Damage, and Commercial Auto & Accidents, the policy covers the risks faced by contractors in various industries.

## Key Features
- **Workers' Compensation**: Provides coverage for medical expenses and lost wages due to injuries and illnesses.
- **Property Coverage**: Protects against damage, loss, or theft of tools, equipment, and vehicles.
- **Professional Liability**: Offers protection against errors and omissions, ensuring your business is defended against client complaints.
- **Commercial Auto & Accidents**: Covers commercial vehicles used for work and protects against accidents.
