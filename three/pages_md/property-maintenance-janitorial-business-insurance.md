# Berkshire Hathaway Property Maintenance & Janitorial Insurance

## Overview
Three by Berkshire Hathaway offers comprehensive insurance coverage for property maintenance and janitorial businesses. This policy provides coverage for various types of liabilities, including business liability errors and omissions, directors and officers, general liability, employment practices liability, fiduciary liability, property fire, theft, flood and water damage, wind and hail, earthquake, equipment breakdown, inland marine business, auto collision, comprehensive auto liability, hired and non-owned business interruption, business income, extra expenses, cyber data breach, cyber liability, workers' compensation, lost wages, medical treatment. 

The policy also includes a no-cost quote feature, allowing customers to get a quick estimate of their insurance needs without the hassle of filling out forms. Additionally, there is a cookie policy and privacy policy that outline how the company handles user data and protects customer information.
