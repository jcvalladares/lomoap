## Berkshire Hathaway Three by Berkshire Hathaway Comprehensive Insurance for Creative Services Business

### Summary
Berkshire Hathaway's Three by Berkshire Hathway is a comprehensive insurance package designed specifically for creative services businesses. The package covers a wide range of potential risks, including business liability, errors and omissions, directors and officers, general liability, employment practices liability, fiduciary liability, property fire, theft, flood and water damage, wind and hail, earthquake, equipment breakdown, inland marine business, auto collision, comprehensive auto liability, hired and non-owned business interruption, business income, extra expenses, cyber data breach, cyber liability, and workers' compensation. It provides comprehensive coverage with no need for multiple policies.

### Key Features
- **Comprehensive Coverage**: One policy covering all specified risks.
- **No Multiple Policies**: Saves time and money on managing multiple policies.
- **Customizable**: Tailored to meet specific needs of creative services businesses.
- **Access to Experts**: Access to experienced agents who can provide personalized advice and support.
