# Three by Berkshire Hathaway

## Overview
Three by Berkshire Hathaway offers a comprehensive business insurance package that covers various aspects of business operations, including liability, cyber, property, and more. It provides up to $5 million in liability limits without the need for multiple policies, making it an affordable option for small businesses.

## Features
- **Comprehensive Coverage**: Offers a wide range of coverages tailored to meet the specific needs of small businesses.
- **No Fine Print**: Removes the fine print from the policy, reducing potential confusion.
- **Fewer Pages**: Simplifies the policy, reducing exclusions.
- **Financial Strength**: Coverage backed by the financial strength and reputation of Berkshire Hathaway.

## Customer Feedback
Many customers have found the insurance coverage provided by Three by Berkshire Hathaway to be quick, helpful, and affordable. They appreciate the comprehensive nature of the policy and the streamlined process for obtaining quotes and setup.
