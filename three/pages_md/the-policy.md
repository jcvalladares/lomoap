### Title: Comprehensive Small Business Insurance Policy

#### Summary:
Three by Berkshire Hathaway offers a three-page policy that provides comprehensive coverage for various types of incidents, liabilities, and property damages, all within a single policy. The policy covers businesses across different industries, including cleaning and janitorial, hospitality, residential construction, restaurants and taverns, technology services, trade contractors, resources, articles and whitepapers, FAQs, certificates, claims, claims process, file a worker's compensation claim, file a property, auto, or liability claim, agents get quote, the policy number, policy period, issued by, named insured, what's insured and for how much, insurance summary page, policy forms and endorsements, reporting a claim or for questions, what we do, what we don't cover, deductibles and limits, what your business needs to do, questions?
