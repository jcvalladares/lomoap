# Restaurant & Hotel Property Insurance - THREE

## Overview

This document provides an overview of the benefits and features of the THREE insurance policy, specifically tailored for hospitality businesses such as restaurants and hotels. It outlines the key aspects of the policy, including the types of coverage included, exclusions, and how it compares to other types of insurance options.

### Key Features of THREE Insurance Policy

1. **Comprehensive Coverage**: The policy covers most property-related incidents a hospitality business is likely to face, including fire, electrical problems, vandalism, theft, accidental damage, food spoilage, equipment breakdown, and more.
   
2. **Industry-Specific Coverage**: The policy is designed to address the unique risks faced by hotels and restaurants, providing specialized coverage for items like equipment, HVAC systems, and laundry equipment.
   
3. **Business Interruption Protection**: The policy covers costs related to keeping the business running after a loss, ensuring that your operations remain operational during an emergency.
   
4. **Data Damage Protection**: The policy pays for incidents affecting property in your company's care, custody, or control.
   
5. **Loss of or Damage to Others' Property**: The policy protects against incidents affecting property in your company's care, custody, or control.

### Exclusions

The policy includes some exclusions, such as occurrences that start or take place outside the policy period, ordinary property wear and tear, lost or misplaced property, anything personal to you (or anyone) independent of your business, any building your business owns, leases, occupies, or rents that isn't listed on the policy, and damage or business interruption caused by pollution, asbestos, or a nuclear event.

Your hotel or restaurant property insurance cost will depend on various factors such as your location, the size of your business, your claims history, and more. To get a precise quote, you can visit the website at [THREE](https://www.three.com/) and follow the instructions provided.
