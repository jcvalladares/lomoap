# General Liability Insurance with THREE

## Overview
Three by Berkshire Hathaway offers comprehensive liability coverage tailored to the unique needs of small businesses across various industries. This policy provides protection against unforeseen claims, ensuring your business remains financially sound and compliant with legal requirements.

### Key Features
1. **Comprehensive Protection**: Covers Bodily Injury, Property Damage, and Personal and Advertising Injury.
2. **Automatic Exclusions**: Clearly stated to avoid surprise claims.
3. **Cyber Liability**: Included in standard coverage.
4. **Professional Liability Insurance**: Essential for consultants and accountants.
5. **Employment Practices Liability Insurance**: Required for safeguarding against discrimination.
6. **Seamless Protection**: Seamless integration of multiple policies for comprehensive coverage.
7. **Flexible Coverage**: Allows businesses to choose which policies best suit their needs.

### Benefits
- **Confidence in Coverage**: Ensures peace of mind with automatic inclusion of key protections.
- **Reduced Costs**: Avoids unnecessary premium increases and reduces the risk of costly claims.
- **Flexibility**: Tailors coverage to meet specific business needs without requiring additional purchasing.
