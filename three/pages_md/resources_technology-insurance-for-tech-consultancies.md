### Title: Key Insights on Tech Insurance for Your Consultancy or Technology Company

#### Summary:
Tech insurance is crucial for startups and professionals in the technology industry. It provides financial protection against various risks such as property damage, client lawsuits, cyber attacks, and employee injuries. This comprehensive insurance policy offers coverage for multiple aspects of a technology company's operations, including Professional Liability, Cyber Liability, and others. Understanding these risks and how to mitigate them is essential for building a robust and secure business.
