# Berkshire Hathaway THREE

## Overview

Three is a comprehensive insurance product offered by Berkshire Hathaway, providing coverage for various industries including construction. It offers various types of policies such as Business Liability Errors & Omissions, Directors & Officers, General Liability, Employment Practices Liability, Fiduciary Liability, Property Fire Theft Flood & Water Damage, Wind & Hail, Earthquake, Equipment Breakdown, Inland Marine Business, Auto Collision, Comprehensive Auto Liability, Hired & Non-Owned Business Interruption, Business Income, Extra Expenses, Cyber Data Breach, Cyber Liability, and Workers' Compensation. The policy covers losses caused by errors or omissions, directors and officers, general liability, employment practices liability, fiduciary liability, property damage, theft, flood, water damage, wind, hail, earthquake, equipment breakdown, inland marine business, auto collision, comprehensive auto liability, hired and non-owned business interruption, business income, extra expenses, cyber data breach, cyber liability, and workers' compensation. 

## Key Features

- **Comprehensive Coverage**: One comprehensive policy covers all the above risks.
- **Multiple Policies**: No need for multiple policies. 
- **No Multiple Premiums**: The premium is based on a single policy.
- **Quick Access**: Online quote service available.
- **Easy to Use**: Easy to navigate and understand.

## How to Apply

To apply for a Three policy, visit the website at [www.three.com](https://www.three.com) and follow the instructions provided. The application process typically involves filling out a form with basic information about the business, selecting the appropriate industry, and providing additional details if necessary.
