# Privacy Policy

## Overview

Three by Berkshire Hathaway, a leading provider of insurance solutions, is committed to protecting the privacy of its customers. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect the personal information that individuals provide to us.

### Collection and Processing of Personal Information

Three collects personal information primarily through direct interactions with its customers, such as when they submit an application for insurance or provide additional information during the policy issuance process. Additionally, Third parties may provide information about the customer's business or personal life, which is collected and processed in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

### Use of Personal Information

The personal information collected by Three is used to determine whether to issue an insurance policy, to serve any policies issued, and to adjust any claims submitted under any policy. The company may also share certain personal information with third parties as permitted or required by law, such as to obtain a credit history necessary for processing the policy application.

### Retention and Protection of Personal Information

Three is required by law to keep personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes for which it is being used. The period for which personal information is kept will be determined by a number of criteria, including the purposes for which the information is being used, the amount and sensitivity of the information, the potential risk from any unauthorized use or disclosure of the information, and our legal and regulatory obligations. To ensure the security of personal information, Three trains its employees to safeguard the information and maintains physical and procedural safeguards to prevent access to the information by third parties. Finally, the use of personal information is protected by applicable law.

### Access and Correction of Personal Information

Individuals can contact Three at 1-800-507-4495 to review or correct their personal information. The company may request proof of identity before disclosing the personal information to the individual or comply with other requests. The company wants to ensure that the personal information it holds about the individual and their preferences as to how they are contacted is accurate and up to date.

### Internet Environment

While Three tries to protect the information provided to it, users should recognize that the transmission of information via the internet is not completely secure. This risk is common across the internet and not specific to their services. Users should take precautions to protect their personal information, such as signing off and closing their browser when they have finished their session. This will help to ensure that others do not access their personal information if they share their computing device or use a computing device in a public place such as a library or internet café. Three also uses technology that includes web beacons to other websites that are not its own. Those websites may place a cookie on the user's computer, which registers their IP address and the websites that address visits. Part of Three's advertising includes remarketing, a technology which allows advertising to people who have previously visited its website when they browse websites across the internet. Three uses that technology, including Google Analytics, to help it measure its website performance or advertise its products and services. Please see the Cookie Policy for more information about how to restrict cookies on your computer. You can also learn about available opt-outs for the web at Google Tools. By using Three's website, you are acknowledging the use of cookies and the collection, sharing, and use of personal data for personalization of ads.

### Legal Compliance

By using Three's website, you agree to the use of cookies and the collection, sharing, and use of personal data for personalization of ads. Three by Berkshire Hathaway reserves the right to modify this policy at any time without notice. Any changes will be posted on the website.
