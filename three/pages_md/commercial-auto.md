### Title: Comprehensive Auto Insurance for Your Business

#### Summary:
Three by Berkshire Hathaway offers a single comprehensive policy that covers all aspects of your business's insurance needs, including comprehensive, collision, auto liability, hired & non-owned auto liability, cargo liability, tools & equipment, workers' compensation, and more. This policy eliminates the need for multiple separate policies and reduces the administrative burden of managing multiple insurance programs.

#### Key Features:
- **Comprehensive Coverage**: Protects against non-collision damages.
- **Collision Protection**: Pays for repairs or replacements when your vehicle is damaged in an accident.
- **Auto Liability**: Provides financial protection in case of accidents involving bodily injury or property damage.
- **Hired & Non-Owned Auto Liability**: Covers rental, leasing, and borrowing vehicles.
- **Cargo Liability**: Designed for carriers transporting goods.
- **Tools & Equipment**: Helps pay for replacement of tools and equipment.
- **Workers' Compensation**: Covers injuries caused by workplace accidents.
