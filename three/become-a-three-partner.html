Exposure & Coverage Limits

THREE has no hard and fast limits on revenue, payroll, or TIV

The following risk characteristics will trigger an underwriting referral for closer review before releasing a quote:

  • Revenue exceeding $6m

  • Payroll exceeding $6m

  • Building values exceeding $3m

  • Building values more than +/- 20% from the pre-filled estimate replacement cost

  • All Other Property values exceeding $1m
