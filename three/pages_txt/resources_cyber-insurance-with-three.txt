As a business owner, you can protect yourself from damage by being prepared with cyber insurance. What is Cyber Insurance? Cyber Insurance is a type of coverage that protects businesses in the event of data breaches, phishing attacks, and other cyber security issues. When a cyberattack results in compromised data, you may be liable to notify affected parties. You may also lose money to a phishing attack or a ransomware demand or have to shut down because of a cyber-related event that prevents you from conducting business as usual.
